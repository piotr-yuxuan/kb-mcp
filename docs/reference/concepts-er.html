ER · kb-mcp conceptual model

The conceptual model, drawn as entities

The twelve core concepts of the concepts page, drawn with every relationship and its cardinality, in two views: what the knowledge is, then how it is kept current and served. Dashed boxes are parts that a core concept defines (a person's mentions, a run's outcomes, a section's chunks), not concepts of their own.

View 1

What the knowledge is

From the kind of origin down to the sections of a document and the people it names. # marks what identifies an entity, → a reference to another.

The headline

Document is the root

A document is a unit of content that an origin holds and a scope lists. Its identity, (source, id), is unique across every site and client. Its parent and its links name other documents by document id, and its mentions name people; all are lines of its front matter. A container, such as a folder or a directory, is a document with no body, and the parents make the scope's tree. Its as-of time is its scope's.

Three levels of origin

Connector is the kind of origin, Source a configured instance of it, Scope the part the user selects. Two Jira sites and three Confluence sites for three clients are five sources of two connectors, and three clients' code bases three sources of a git connector: values, not new concepts. A source's way to sign in is defined by its connector.

Mention, the evidence for a person

A mention is one role that one document gives to one name: the origin's fields (author, creator, assignee), activity (commenter), attendance (participant), or the text (tagged, which no connector extracts). It carries an identity qualified by its issuer, when the origin gives one, and the spelling. Persons are joined from the mentions of every source by five rules, in order: identity, alias, name key, attendance, name. Two identities are one person only when an alias says so.

View 2

How it is kept current and served

A run executes the pipeline's stages, which move data between stores; tools answer agents from the index. The grey box is drawn in view 1 and repeated here as an anchor.

The headline

A run is what the program does for one command

Its configuration is fixed when it starts, whichever layers gave it: the stages enabled, the sources and scopes selected, every relative value made absolute; secrets are never kept. Its units are a stage applied to one source, or over the whole; only stages depend on one another. It is idempotent and resumable by its id. Of the copy stores and the mirror it changes only its scopes; the index, persons and vectors follow the whole mirror.

Stage is a move between stores

Fetch, prepare, index and embed each read stores and write one, redoing only what changed. Stores are of six sorts: copy, derived, memo, input, credential and journal; a derivation's fingerprint keys what it wrote. Of the stages, embed alone calls a service that computes. The sign-in state, a credential store, is written by login, a run of its own, before the runs that rely on it.

Retrieval returns a ranking

A ranking is a sequence of sections, at most one per document, best first or by date; one found by meaning names the chunk that matched. It records its mode and whether it was reranked, and holds no score and nothing of its display. A tool shows each ranked section as a passage: a snippet in search, a quote in ask.
