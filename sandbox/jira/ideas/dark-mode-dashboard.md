## Problem

Merchants who work night shifts have asked for a dark theme since 2024. The design
system now supports it, as [Theming and dark mode](../../corpus/design/theming.md)
describes, but Merchant Hub pages built before Inkwell 3 still hard-code colours.

## Idea

Finish the migration of the remaining pages to tokens, then offer the dark theme to all
merchants, following the operating system's setting by default.

## Evidence

Eleven of the 24 merchants interviewed in May 2026 mentioned eye strain during evening
reconciliation.
