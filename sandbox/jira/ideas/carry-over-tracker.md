## Problem

Employees cannot see how many days they will carry into next year, or by when they must
use them. The rules differ between the [UK policy](../../corpus/people/annual-leave-uk.md)
and the [French one](../../corpus/people/conges-payes-rtt.md), and the People team
answers the same question by email every December.

## Idea

A carry-over line in Haven's leave summary, computed from each country's rules, with a
reminder a month before the deadline.

Raised by Rhiannon Dace.
