Welcome to the Quillmoor handbook, the company wiki. Each section is kept by the team whose work it describes; the owner of each page is named on it.

- [Payments platform](payments/index.md): Runbooks, architecture and team notes for card authorisation, payouts, the ledger and settlement.
- [Release engineering](release/index.md): How changes reach production, and how to undo or pause them safely.
- [Incidents and on-call](incidents/index.md): How we run production incidents and on-call, with the reports of past incidents.
- [Security](security/index.md): Security policies, standards and playbooks for incidents, secrets, phishing and access.
- [Data platform](data/index.md): The data platform, its pipelines, and the rules for working with warehouse data.
- [Machine learning](ml/index.md): How the Risk ML team trains, evaluates, ships and monitors the fraud model.
- [Design system](design/index.md): Inkwell, our design system: tokens, components, accessibility and theming.
- [People and HR](people/index.md): Policies and guides from the People team, for the UK and France.
- [Legal](legal/index.md): Contract templates, signature rules and company policies, from the Legal team.
- [Workplace](workplace/index.md): Our offices in London and Paris: getting in, booking a desk, hosting visitors and travelling.
- [Customer support](support/index.md): Playbooks and guides for the Merchant Support team.
- [Finance](finance/index.md): Budgets, approvals, expenses and cost allocation, from the Finance team.
- [Architecture decisions](architecture/index.md): Architecture decision records, from the first to the latest.
- [Engineering handbook](engineering/index.md): How we write, review, test and ship code, with the guild and retrospective notes.
- [Product and roadmap](product/index.md): The product roadmap, the planning process, product briefs and release notes.
