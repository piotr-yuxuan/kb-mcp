# VAT on expenses

| Owner | Last reviewed |
|---|---|
| Henrietta Quaile, finance controller | 2026-04-22 |

Quillmoor is registered for VAT in the UK, which means we can recover the VAT on most of
the business costs we pay, including those you pay and claim back. At the standard rate
of 20%, VAT is a sixth of a receipt's total, so getting it right matters: in FY2025/26
we recovered £41,300 of VAT on expenses and card spending alone.

You do not need to know the VAT rules. You need to keep the right receipt and choose
the right category in Tally; Finance does the rest.

## What you need to do

1. **Keep a VAT receipt** whenever the supplier gives one. Ask for it at the till, as
   it is rarely possible to get one afterwards.
2. **Choose the correct category** in Tally. The category tells Tally whether VAT can be
   recovered at all.
3. **Check the VAT amount** that Tally reads from the receipt. If the receipt shows no
   VAT number, leave the VAT field at zero, even if the receipt mentions VAT.

## What makes a valid VAT receipt

A receipt lets us recover VAT only if it shows:

- the supplier's name and address;
- the supplier's VAT registration number;
- the date;
- a description of what was bought;
- the VAT rate, and the VAT amount or enough to work it out.

For a total of up to £250 including VAT, a simplified receipt with those details is
enough. Above £250, ask for a full VAT invoice addressed to Quillmoor Ltd. Hotels issue
one on request at checkout.

## Category by category

| Cost in the UK | Can we recover VAT? |
|---|---|
| Hotels | Yes, with a VAT invoice |
| Rail fares | No VAT to recover: fares are zero-rated |
| Flights | No VAT to recover on the fare |
| Taxis | Only when the receipt shows the driver's or operator's VAT number |
| Per diems | No: a per diem is not a purchase and carries no VAT |
| Meals while working late, team meals | Yes, with a VAT receipt |
| Entertaining merchants, partners or suppliers | No: VAT on business entertainment cannot be recovered |
| Mileage in your own car | Partly, through the fuel element; keep a fuel receipt dated before the journey |
| Training courses and books | Courses usually yes; printed books carry no VAT |
| Parking | Yes for car parks with a VAT receipt; on-street parking carries no VAT |

## Costs outside the UK

VAT or sales tax paid in another country cannot be recovered through Tally. Enter such
costs with zero VAT. When a team plans a large event abroad, with more than £5,000 of
costs, tell the finance controller beforehand: for events of that size we sometimes
make a separate claim to the other country's tax authority, which needs proper invoices
in Quillmoor's name.

Colleagues employed in France follow French rules on TVA, which are set out on
[notes de frais](notes-de-frais.md).

## Corporate cards

The same rules apply to card transactions. Attach the VAT receipt to the transaction in
Tally exactly as you would for a claim; see [corporate cards](corporate-cards.md).

## Common mistakes

- Typing the total into the net field, which makes Tally calculate VAT on top of VAT.
- Entering VAT from a receipt that has no VAT number.
- Recovering VAT on an entertainment dinner by choosing "team meal" as the category.
  If merchants or suppliers were at the table, it is entertainment.

The general rules for what you may claim are in the [expenses policy](expenses.md).
