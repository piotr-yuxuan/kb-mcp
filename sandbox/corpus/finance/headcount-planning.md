# Headcount planning

Owners: Leontine Barrow (financial planning lead) and Rhiannon Dace (head of people).
Last reviewed 2026-08-27.

People are about two thirds of Quillmoor's costs, so the headcount plan is the largest
single decision in each year's budget. This page explains how roles are planned and
approved, how we cost them, and what to do when the plan and reality part ways.

## What counts as headcount

Headcount means people on Quillmoor's payroll, permanent or on a fixed-term contract,
in the UK or in France. Contractors, agency staff and consultants are not headcount:
they are bought through purchase orders under the [spend approval matrix](spend-approval.md),
and they sit in the non-headcount lines of a budget.

## Positions

Each role in the approved budget is a **position**, with a number in the form `POS-`
followed by five digits, held in the planning model. A position records the cost centre,
the level, the location, the planned start month and whether it is a new role or a
replacement.

People opens a recruitment requisition only against a position number. If a hiring
manager asks a recruiter to start on a role without one, the recruiter will send them
here.

## The plan in the budget cycle

Budget holders build the headcount plan for next year in November and December, role by
role, and agree it with People by 12 December. It then goes into the draft budget due on
15 January, and through the challenge sessions and board approval described on the
[annual budget cycle](budget-cycle.md).

## How a role is costed

The planning model turns a base salary into a fully loaded annual cost with a single
rate per country:

| Employed in | Fully loaded cost |
|---|---|
| UK | Base salary × 1.28 |
| France | Base salary × 1.45 |

The rate covers employer's taxes and social charges, pension, benefits and an allowance
for equipment. It does not cover recruitment fees, which are budgeted separately, or
travel, which follows the per-head assumption in the budget. Salary bands come from
People; do not enter a figure above the band's midpoint without People's agreement.

## Changes during the year

| Situation | What to do | Who approves |
|---|---|---|
| Someone leaves and you want to replace them at the same level | Tell People; the position reopens | The budget holder |
| Replacement at a higher level | Update the position in the planning model | The finance director |
| A budgeted role starts earlier than planned | Tell financial planning so the forecast moves | No approval needed |
| A role that is not in the budget | Write a one-page business case for financial planning | The CFO, after the finance director's review |
| Converting a contractor into an employee | Treat as a new role unless a position is free | As for a new role |

Unbudgeted roles are not impossible, but the business case must say where the money
comes from: savings elsewhere in the directorate, a vacancy that will not be filled, or
growth that the reforecast will show.

## Internal moves

When someone moves team, their cost moves to the new cost centre on the first day of the
following month. The new manager tells People in Haven, and People tells financial
planning. If the move leaves a gap the old team wants to fill, that is a replacement and
follows the table above.

## Reporting

At each month-end close, financial planning reconciles the people on payroll in Haven
with the positions in the planning model, by working day 3. Each budget holder's monthly
pack lists filled positions, open positions and anyone on payroll without a position;
the last should never happen, and finance follows each case up.

## Example

Payouts loses an engineer in August. Yusuf Achterberg, budget holder for `CC-3200`,
decides to replace the role at the same level, so the position reopens without further
approval. The base salary is £85,000, giving a fully loaded cost of £108,800 a year.
Recruitment takes longer than hoped and the new engineer starts on 1 October, so the cost
for the rest of the fiscal year, October to March, is six months: £54,400. Financial
planning updates the forecast for the two months the position stood empty.
