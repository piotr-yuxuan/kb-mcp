# Travel per diems

Owner: Henrietta Quaile, finance controller. Last reviewed 2026-07-01.

A per diem is a flat daily amount that covers your meals and small incidental costs
while you travel for work. You claim it in Tally instead of claiming individual meal
receipts. It applies to people employed in the UK; colleagues employed in France are
reimbursed for meals at cost, as described on [notes de frais](notes-de-frais.md).

Hotels, transport and taxis are not part of the per diem. Their rules and caps are in the
[expenses policy](expenses.md).

## Rates

| Destination | Day trip of more than 5 hours | Each day with an overnight stay |
|---|---|---|
| United Kingdom | £12 | £45 |
| Paris | €20 | €60 |
| Elsewhere in France | €18 | €50 |
| Ireland | €20 | €65 |
| Other EU countries | €18 | €55 |
| Rest of the world | As shown in Tally for the city | As shown in Tally for the city |

A day trip counts when you are away from your usual place of work for more than 5 hours
and the trip is not to another Quillmoor office. Working a day in the Paris office as a
London employee, with no overnight stay, does not earn a per diem.

Overseas rates are paid in pounds, converted at Tally's rate on the first day of the
trip.

## How the days are counted

- **Days with an overnight stay** earn the overnight rate, including the day you travel
  out.
- **The day you return** earns the overnight rate if you arrive home after 19:00, and the
  day-trip rate otherwise.
- **Breakfast.** If breakfast is not included in your room, add £8 in the UK or €10
  abroad for each morning. If it is included, add nothing.

## When meals are provided

If someone else provides a meal, such as a conference lunch or a merchant's dinner,
reduce the day's rate:

| Meal provided | Reduction |
|---|---|
| Lunch | 30% of the day's rate |
| Dinner | 40% of the day's rate |
| Lunch and dinner | 70% of the day's rate |

Tally applies the reductions when you tick the meals that were provided.

## Claiming

1. In Tally, choose **New expense > Per diem**.
2. Enter the destination, the dates and times you left and returned, and tick any meals
   that were provided.
3. Tally calculates the amount. No receipts are needed for per diems.
4. Submit within the same deadline as any other claim, and your manager approves as
   usual.

## Example

A London engineer spends Monday to Wednesday at a merchant's office in Edinburgh,
leaving home at 07:00 on Monday and arriving home at 17:30 on Wednesday. Breakfast is
included at the hotel, and the merchant provides lunch on Tuesday.

| Day | Rate | Reduction | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Overnight, £45 | None | £45.00 |
| Tuesday | Overnight, £45 | Lunch, 30% | £31.50 |
| Wednesday | Day trip, £12 (home before 19:00) | None | £12.00 |
| **Total** | | | **£88.50** |

## Questions

Unusual trips, such as long stays of more than four weeks in one place, have different
arrangements; ask the finance controller before you travel.
