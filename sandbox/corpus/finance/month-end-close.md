# Month-end close calendar

Owner: Henrietta Quaile, finance controller. Last reviewed: 2026-10-02.

Quillmoor's books for a month are **closed by the fifth working day** of the following
month. After that the month is locked in the accounting system: nothing can be posted to
it, and corrections go into the next month. Working days are those of the London office,
so UK bank holidays do not count; the Paris office follows the same calendar for the
close.

## What happens when

| Working day | What happens | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 3 before month end | Accrual requests sent to budget holders, with their open purchase orders | Finance controller |
| Last working day | Last supplier payment run of the month | Accounts payable |
| 1 | Bank statements loaded; reconciliation results reviewed with the Ledger team | Finance controller |
| 2, by 12:00 | **Cut-off** for expense claims, card transactions and goods received in Tally | Everyone |
| 3 | Accruals, prepayments, the payroll journal and recharges between the UK and France posted | Finance team |
| 4 | Draft management accounts; first variance review | Financial planning |
| 5 | Books closed and locked, after the CFO's review | Finance controller |
| 7 | Budget against actuals packs sent to budget holders | Financial planning |

## What we need from you

- **Approve expense claims and code card transactions by 12:00 on working day 2.**
  Anything approved later lands in the following month. That does not delay anyone's
  reimbursement, but it does distort your cost centre's month. See the
  [expenses policy](expenses.md) and [corporate cards](corporate-cards.md).
- **Mark goods and services as received in Tally** when they arrive, not when the
  invoice does. Receipts drive our accruals.
- **Answer accrual requests by working day 1.** If you have used a service in the month
  and the invoice has not arrived, tell us the amount; an estimate is far better than
  nothing.
- **Tell us about anything unusual** before the end of the month: a large prepayment, a
  supplier dispute, a contract ending early.

## Dependencies on other teams

The close relies on the Ledger team's nightly reconciliation being clean, or its
breaks explained, by working day 1; the process is on
[ledger reconciliation breaks](../payments/ledger-reconciliation.md). Several finance
reports also read from warehouse marts maintained by Data Platform. If a finance mart is
late during the close, Data Platform handles it under its
[data quality alerts](../data/quality-alerts.md) process, and the finance controller
joins the incident channel.

## The year-end close

March is closed on the same five-day timetable for the management accounts. The
statutory accounts follow later, with the auditors, and any audit adjustments are
posted to a separate year-end period so that the March management accounts are never
reopened. The timetable for the audit is shared with budget holders in February, during
the [annual budget cycle](budget-cycle.md).
