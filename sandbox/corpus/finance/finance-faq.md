# Finance FAQ

The questions the finance team is asked most often in `#ask-finance`, with short answers
and a link to the page that holds the detail. If the answer here and the linked page ever
disagree, the linked page wins; please tell us so we can fix this one.

*Curated by the finance team. Last reviewed 2026-10-15.*

## Spending money

**I want to buy something for my team. Where do I start?**
With a purchase order in Tally, before you commit to anything. The steps are on
[raising a purchase order in Tally](purchase-orders.md), and who approves at which value
is on the [spend approval matrix](spend-approval.md).

**It is only a small software tool. Surely that does not need a purchase order?**
Software is the one category where size does not matter: every tool, free ones
included, needs Security's review before anyone uses it with company data. Raise the
purchase order and tick the software box; Tally does the rest.

**Who signs the contract once my purchase order is approved?**
Not necessarily the person who approved the spend. Check
[contract signature authority](../legal/contract-signature-authority.md), and send the
contract to Legal through Clausebook as described on
[requesting a contract review](../legal/contract-requests.md).

**Can I just put it on my corporate card?**
For small, one-off business costs, yes. Not for software, and not for anything that
should be invoiced. See [corporate cards](corporate-cards.md).

## Expenses and travel

**How do I claim back something I paid for?**
In Tally. The rules, limits and examples are in the [expenses policy](expenses.md) if
you are employed in the UK, and in [notes de frais](notes-de-frais.md) if you are
employed in France.

**Do I need to keep meal receipts on a business trip?**
Not if you are employed in the UK: overnight trips are covered by a flat daily rate,
described on [travel per diems](per-diems.md). Colleagues employed in France claim meals
at cost, with receipts.

**When will my expenses be paid?**
UK claims are paid in the weekly run on Thursdays; French claims on Tuesdays. Both pages
above give the cut-off times.

**Is my salary paid by Finance?**
Payroll is run by People, through Haven. The dates are on
[payroll dates](../people/payroll-dates.md).

**I have a course I would like to take. Is that an expense?**
It is claimed in Tally like an expense but charged to your
[learning budget](../people/learning-budget.md). Agree it with your manager first.

**A merchant invited me to dinner. Do I need to tell anyone?**
Possibly: the [gifts and hospitality](../legal/gifts-and-hospitality.md) policy says when
gifts and hospitality must be registered.

## Budgets and cost centres

**Which cost centre am I on?**
Your team's. The full list, with budget holders, is on the
[cost centre list](cost-centres.md).

**How do I see what my team has spent?**
Budget holders receive a pack every month, on the seventh working day, showing actuals
against budget and forecast. Cloud costs are also visible daily on the Lantern dashboard
"Cloud costs by cost centre"; see [cloud cost allocation](cloud-cost-allocation.md).

**When is next year's budget set?**
Drafts are prepared over the winter and approved by the board before the fiscal year
starts. The timetable is on the [annual budget cycle](budget-cycle.md).

**We need to hire someone who is not in the budget. What now?**
Read [headcount planning](headcount-planning.md) before you talk to a recruiter.

**Why is there a cost on my cost centre that I do not recognise?**
Look at the description in the monthly pack first. Cloud costs are allocated by rules
that sometimes surprise people; the rules are on the cloud cost allocation page. If it
still makes no sense, ask in `#ask-finance` with the cost centre and the amount.

## Suppliers and invoices

**A supplier says they have not been paid.**
Ask in `#ask-finance` with the supplier's name and invoice number. The most common
causes are an invoice sent to an individual instead of `finance@quillmoor.example`, a
missing purchase order number, or goods not marked as received in Tally. See
[paying suppliers](paying-suppliers.md).

**A supplier emailed to say their bank details have changed.**
Do not act on it and do not reply. Forward it to `finance@quillmoor.example`. We only
change bank details after a call-back to a contact we already know.

**What VAT number do I give a supplier?**
Suppliers who need it should ask accounts payable directly. Our company details are on
every purchase order Tally sends.

## The month-end close

**Why does Finance chase me at the start of every month?**
Because the books close by the fifth working day and we need your approvals, receipts
and accruals before then. The [month-end close calendar](month-end-close.md) shows what
we need and when.

**I missed the cut-off. Is that a problem?**
Not for anyone's reimbursement. The cost simply lands in the following month, which
makes your monthly figures look odd, so add a note in your commentary.

## Still stuck?

Ask in `#ask-finance`, which the team watches during London business hours, or write to
`finance@quillmoor.example`. For anything confidential, such as a concern about a claim
or a payment, write directly to the finance controller or the finance director.
