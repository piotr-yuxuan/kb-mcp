# Expenses policy

|---|---|
| Applies to | Everyone employed by Quillmoor in the UK, and contractors whose contract allows expenses |

| Last reviewed | 2026-07-01 |
| Contact | `finance@quillmoor.example`, or `#ask-finance` in chat |

If you are employed in France, this page does not apply to you: read
[Notes de frais (France)](notes-de-frais.md), which has its own limits.

## The short version

- Claim in **Tally**, **within 60 days** of spending the money.
- Attach an itemised **receipt for anything above £10**.
- Take public transport. **Taxis are for journeys after 21:00**, and for the few
  exceptions in section 5.
- Hotels are **capped at £180 a night in London and £140 a night elsewhere in the UK**.
- Meals on overnight trips are covered by [travel per diems](per-diems.md), not by
  receipts.
- If you hold a corporate card, use it for business costs and reconcile it in Tally.

The rest of this page explains the detail, with examples. When the policy does not
answer your question, ask before you spend, not after.

## 1. Principles

We reimburse costs that are necessary for your work, reasonable in amount and incurred
wholly for Quillmoor's business. We would rather you asked than guessed, and we will
never mind a question. Three tests help when a case is not covered below:

1. Would you be comfortable if the claim appeared, with your name, in the monthly
   report that the finance director reads? (It might.)
2. Would a colleague doing the same trip have spent roughly the same?
3. Was there a cheaper option that would have done the job just as well?

## 2. Claiming in Tally

1. Open Tally and choose **New expense**, or forward a receipt to the address shown
   under your profile; Tally reads the receipt and starts a draft.
2. Pick the category (travel, hotel, per diem, meals, training, other). The category
   sets the account code and the VAT treatment, so choose carefully.
3. Choose the cost centre. It defaults to your own; change it only when your budget
   holder has agreed that another cost centre pays, and say who agreed.
4. Attach the receipt, enter the amount in the currency you paid and add a sentence
   saying what the cost was for and, for meals, who was there.
5. Submit. Your line manager approves in Tally; managers are asked to approve within 5
   business days.

You must submit a claim **within 60 days** of the date on the receipt. After 60 days
Tally stops accepting the claim, and only the finance controller can reopen it, which
she does for real reasons such as long leave or a lost card statement, not for
forgetfulness.

Approved claims are paid by bank transfer in the weekly expenses run every Thursday.
A claim approved by 17:00 on Tuesday is in that week's run. Claims in other currencies
are converted at Tally's rate on the day of the expense, and the converted amount is
what you receive.

> [!TIP]
> Submit as you go rather than saving claims up for the end of a trip. Short claims are
> approved faster, and you will not lose the receipts.

## 3. Receipts

You need an itemised receipt for any expense **above £10**. A card terminal slip that
shows only a total is not itemised; ask for the full receipt. For £10 or less a
description is enough, although a receipt is always welcome.

If you lose a receipt for something above £10, complete the missing receipt declaration
in Tally. Each declaration needs your manager's approval, and Finance follows up with
anyone who makes more than two in a fiscal year.

For UK purchases, a VAT receipt that shows the supplier's VAT number lets us recover the
VAT. The rules on what counts as a valid VAT receipt are on
[VAT on expenses](vat-on-expenses.md); you do not need to calculate anything, only to
keep the right piece of paper.

## 4. Booking travel

Book trains, flights and hotels through the company's travel booking service whenever
you can; the process is on [booking business travel](../workplace/business-travel.md).
Bookings made there are paid centrally and never appear in your expenses. Claim only
what you had to pay yourself.

| Travel | What we pay |
|---|---|
| Rail in the UK | Standard class, booked ahead where you can |
| Rail abroad | Standard class, or the cheapest fully flexible fare when plans may change |
| Flights up to 6 hours | Economy |
| Flights over 6 hours | Premium economy, with your budget holder's approval in advance |
| Your own car | 45p a mile for business miles, minus your normal commute |
| Bicycle | 20p a mile |
| Parking and tolls | At cost, with a receipt above £10 |

If you combine a business trip with annual leave, we pay the fare we would have paid for
the business trip alone, and nothing for the extra nights.

Travelling with a laptop or phone abroad has its own rules: see
[travelling with company devices](../security/travel-with-devices.md).

## 5. Taxis

Public transport is the default. A taxi is reimbursed:

- for a journey **after 21:00**, for example going home after working late or arriving
  on a late train;
- when you are carrying equipment that would be impractical on public transport;
- when you have a medical or mobility reason, which you need not explain in Tally
  beyond saying "mobility";
- when there is no reasonable public transport, such as an early start at a site
  outside a town.

"After 21:00" means the journey starts after 21:00. A taxi booked at 20:30 to beat the
rain does not qualify, however late the evening felt. Take the standard option, not the
premium one, and add a short reason to the claim.

If you are on call and are called out at night, the taxi rule above still applies; the
on-call arrangements themselves are on [on-call](../incidents/on-call.md).

## 6. Hotels

| Location | Cap per night |
|---|---|
| London (inside the M25) | £180 |
| Elsewhere in the UK | £140 |
| Outside the UK | The cap that Tally shows for the destination city |

The cap is for the room, including taxes, and excluding breakfast; breakfast is part of
the per diem when it is not included in the room rate. Book through the travel booking
service, which shows only rooms within the cap by default.

If no room is available within the cap, for instance during a large conference, ask
your budget holder to approve a cap exception in Tally before you book. Without an
approved exception, the amount above the cap is not reimbursed.

We do not pay for upgrades, mini-bars, in-room films, laundry on trips of fewer than
five nights, or a second night when the first was enough.

## 7. Meals and entertaining

- **On a business trip**, meals are covered by the flat daily rate on
  [travel per diems](per-diems.md). Do not claim meal receipts as well.
- **Working late in the office** past 20:00: a meal up to £15, with a receipt above £10.
- **Team meals** paid from a team's budget: up to £30 a head, approved in advance by the
  budget holder, who is usually at the table and cannot approve their own claim.
- **Entertaining merchants, partners or suppliers**: keep it modest and record it as the
  [gifts and hospitality](../legal/gifts-and-hospitality.md) policy requires. List the
  guests and their organisations in the claim.

Alcohol is reimbursed only as part of a meal and in moderation. We never reimburse
spirits or a bar bill without food.

## 9. What we do not pay

- Fines of any kind, including parking and speeding fines.
- Personal travel insurance: our business travel insurance covers you; details are on
  the travel booking page.
- Clothing, grooming and personal items, unless a lost bag means you need essentials
  for a business trip; then reasonable essentials up to £100.
- Gifts to colleagues, including leaving gifts, which come from a team's budget.
- Childcare, pet care and similar costs at home.
- Costs that someone else is paying, such as a conference that includes dinner.

## 10. Corporate cards

If you hold a [corporate card](corporate-cards.md), use it for travel and other business
costs rather than paying yourself and claiming back. Card transactions appear in Tally
automatically; you add the receipt and the description, just as for a claim. The receipt
and approval rules on this page apply to card spending in the same way.

## 11. Worked examples

**A two-day visit to a merchant in Manchester.**

| Item | Paid | Reimbursed | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Return train, standard class, booked through the travel service | £0 to you | n/a | Paid centrally |
| Hotel, one night | £152 | £140 | Above the £140 cap outside London; no exception was asked for |
| Per diem, two days with an overnight stay | n/a | Per the per diem table | No meal receipts needed |
| Taxi from the station to the hotel at 22:15 | £18.40 | £18.40 | After 21:00, receipt attached |
| Coffee at the station | £4.20 | £4.20 | £10 or less, description only |

The £12 above the hotel cap would have been reimbursed if the budget holder had approved
an exception before booking.

**A late night in the London office.**
You work on a release until 21:40 and take a taxi home for £27. The journey started after
21:00, so it is reimbursed with its receipt. Had you left at 20:45 and still taken a
taxi, it would not have been. If you also ordered dinner at 20:15 for £13.50, that is
within the £15 late-working meal and needs its receipt, since it is above £10.

**A three-night conference in London for a colleague from Bristol.**
The conference hotel charges £235 a night. The colleague's budget holder approves a cap
exception in Tally beforehand, giving the reason "conference hotel, no rooms within cap
within walking distance". All three nights are reimbursed in full. Without the
exception, £55 a night would have been the colleague's to pay.

## 12. Mistakes and misuse

Mistakes happen and are simply corrected: Finance rejects the line with a comment and
you resubmit. Deliberately false or inflated claims are a disciplinary matter, and
Quillmoor will recover any amount paid. Approving a claim you know to be wrong is
treated the same way as making it.

Finance samples approved claims every month. A sampled claim may come back to you with
a question weeks after it was paid; please answer it, as the sample is part of our
audit trail.

## 13. Frequently asked questions

**I forgot to claim a train fare from three months ago. Can I still claim it?**
Not normally: the limit is 60 days. Ask the finance controller if there was a real
reason for the delay.

**My hotel was £185 in London. Do I lose the whole amount?**
No. You are reimbursed up to the £180 cap and pay the £5 above it, unless an exception
was approved before you booked.

**Does the taxi rule apply at weekends?**
Yes. The time of the journey is what counts, not the day.

**Can I claim for a colleague?**
No. Each person claims their own expenses, except a meal for a group, which the most
senior person present pays and claims, listing everyone.

**A supplier invoiced me personally. Can I put it through expenses?**
No. Ask the supplier to invoice Quillmoor against a purchase order instead; see
[raising a purchase order in Tally](purchase-orders.md).

**I am visiting the Paris office. Whose rules apply?**
Yours: if you are employed in the UK, this page applies wherever you travel, and the
Tally cap for Paris applies to your hotel.
