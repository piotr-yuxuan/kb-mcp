# Corporate cards

*Maintained by Henrietta Quaile, finance controller. Last reviewed 2026-05-18.*

A corporate card lets you pay for business costs without using your own money. The
card is issued by our card provider in your name, but the account is Quillmoor's: you
are never personally liable for genuine business spending, and you must never use it for
anything else.

## Who can have one

Cards are issued to people who regularly travel or buy for a team, typically:

- anyone who travels for work more than four times a year;
- team leads and budget holders;
- office managers in London and Paris;
- people who regularly book events or venues.

Request a card in Tally under **Cards > Request a card**. Your budget holder approves
the request and Finance orders the card, which arrives at your office within about
ten business days. You activate it in the card provider's app, which is also where you
see your PIN and freeze the card if you need to.

## Limits

| Limit | Standard card | Higher-limit card |
|---|---|---|
| Per transaction | £1,000 | £5,000 |
| Per month | £3,000 | £15,000 |
| Cash withdrawals | Blocked | Blocked |

Higher limits need the finance director's approval and are usually given to office
managers and people who book events. A temporary increase for a single trip can be
requested in Tally and lasts 30 days.

## Using the card

Every transaction appears in Tally within a day or two. For each one, you:

1. attach the receipt, following the receipt rules in the [expenses policy](expenses.md);
2. choose the category and check the cost centre;
3. add a short description of what it was for.

Your line manager then approves it, as for an expense claim. Transactions from a month
must be completed and approved by 12:00 on the second working day of the next month, the
cut-off in the [month-end close calendar](month-end-close.md).

The card is for the same costs that the expenses policy allows, with the same caps on
hotels and the same rule on taxis. It is not a way around the policy.

## What not to put on the card

- **Software, subscriptions and online services.** They need Security's review whatever
  the amount and however they are paid; see the [spend approval matrix](spend-approval.md).
  A card that is used for an unreviewed subscription is suspended until the subscription
  is cancelled or reviewed.
- **Suppliers who should be invoicing us.** Anything recurring or above the card's
  transaction limit belongs on a purchase order.
- **Personal spending**, even if you intend to pay it back. If it happens by mistake,
  mark the transaction as personal in Tally and repay it by bank transfer within 14
  days; Finance will send the details.

## Lost or stolen cards

Freeze the card in the card provider's app straight away, then write to
`finance@quillmoor.example`. If you see a transaction you do not recognise, freeze the
card first and tell us second; we will dispute the transaction with the card provider
for you.

## When you change role or leave

If your new role does not need a card, Finance cancels it. When you leave Quillmoor,
complete all outstanding transactions in Tally before your last day; the card is
cancelled on that day as part of the leaving process described on
[leaving Quillmoor](../people/leaving.md). Uncompleted transactions are followed up with
your manager.

## Repeated problems

Finance reviews card use monthly. Transactions left without receipts for more than 30
days, repeated personal use or spending outside the policy lead first to a reminder, then
to suspension of the card, and in the end to its withdrawal.
