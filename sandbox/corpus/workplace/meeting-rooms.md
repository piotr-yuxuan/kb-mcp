# Meeting rooms

Maintained by the Workplace team. Last reviewed 2026-10-14, after phase 2 of the floor 5 works began.

Every meeting room in Quill House and Maison Lanterne is booked in Perch, the same tool we use for desks and visitors. Rooms follow their own rules, which differ from the desk rules in [Desk booking and hybrid working](desk-booking.md).

## Booking rules

- **Book in Perch**, or from your calendar by adding the room as a guest; both end up in the same place.
- **Book up to 12 weeks ahead.** Recurring bookings are allowed, and Perch asks the organiser to confirm them at the start of each quarter. A recurring booking that is not confirmed lapses.
- **Check in at the panel** by the door within 10 minutes of the start. If nobody does, the room is released for the rest of that slot.
- **On team days**, Tuesday and Thursday, rooms with eight or more seats can be booked for at most two hours at a time.
- **Pick the right size.** Please do not book a ten-seat room for a one-to-one; use a booth or a small room.
- **End five minutes early** so that the next group can get in, and leave the room as you found it: whiteboards wiped, chairs back, cups to the kitchen.

## Equipment

Every room has a screen, a camera, a microphone and a speaker. Share your screen from a company laptop over `QM-Staff`, or use the display cable on the table. The larger rooms also have a whiteboard, and Foolscap and Folio in London and Plume in Paris have a hearing loop.

If something does not work, tell reception, and post the room name in `#workplace-london` or `#bureau-paris` so the next group knows.

### Sepia, the incident room

Anyone can book Sepia, but an incident always takes priority. If the room is needed for one, you will be asked to move, and reception will help you find another room. The way incidents are run is described in [Production incident management](../incidents/incident-management.md).

### The Commons

The Commons on floor 3 is not a meeting room and cannot be booked in Perch. For an all-hands, a talk, a training session or any gathering of more than 20 people, email `workplace@quillmoor.example` with the date, the numbers and what you need. Workplace handles the layout, the microphones and the screens.

## Food and drink for meetings

Order catering by email to `workplace@quillmoor.example` before 12:00 two working days ahead, with the room, the time, the numbers and any dietary needs. Workplace charges it to your team's cost centre. Hospitality for guests from outside the company is covered by [Gifts and hospitality](../legal/gifts-and-hospitality.md).

## Meetings with visitors

Book the room in Perch, and register your visitors separately, at least 24 hours ahead; see [Visitors and deliveries](visitors-and-deliveries.md). In London, use the guest rooms on floor 3 where you can, so that visitors stay close to reception.
