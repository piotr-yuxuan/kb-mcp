# Lost property

Run by reception in London and by Élodie Vasseur in Paris. Last reviewed 2026-07-22.

## You found something

Hand it in to Quillmoor reception on floor 3 of Quill House, or to Élodie at the 4th-floor reception desk in Maison Lanterne. Say where and when you found it. Please do not leave items on a desk or post a photo of someone's wallet in chat.

Reception logs each item with the date, the place it was found and a short description, and keeps it in the lost property cupboard. Wallets, keys, cash, bank cards and jewellery go in the reception safe.

Some things are handled differently:

- **Company laptops, phones and security keys** are handed to the Security team the same day. If you lose one of your own, do not wait for it to turn up here: follow the [Security FAQ](../security/security-faq.md) straight away.
- **Badges** are switched off and returned to their owner, who is usually easy to find.
- **Papers** that look confidential go to Gwen Talbot or Élodie Vasseur, who return them to the owner or shred them.
- **Food and drink** go in the bin at the end of the day.
- **Things found in the lobby, the lifts or another company's floor** belong to the building, not to us. Hand them to the building's security desk, or the ground-floor reception in Paris.

## You lost something

1. Check with reception, in person or at `workplace@quillmoor.example`, with a description and the day you lost it.
2. If it is not there, post in `#workplace-london` or `#bureau-paris`. Someone may still have it at their desk.
3. If you think you lost it in the lobby or a lift, ask the building's security desk as well.

To collect an item, you may be asked to describe it. Valuables are only handed back in person.

## How long we keep things

We keep found items for 30 days. After that, clothing, bags, umbrellas and water bottles are given to a local charity shop, and anything else is recycled or disposed of safely. Cash that nobody claims is given to the charity chosen by staff each year.

## What turns up most often

Umbrellas, water bottles, headphones, chargers and keys, roughly in that order. Chargers and cables that nobody claims within 30 days go into the spare-cable box in the post and print room on floor 3 in London, or next to the printer in Paris, for anyone to borrow. If you are missing a charger, look there before you order a new one.

## Lockers and parcels

The day lockers are emptied every Friday evening at closing time, 21:00 in London and 20:00 in Paris. Their contents come to lost property and are kept for 30 days like everything else.

Parcels that are not collected within five working days, and cannot be returned to the sender, also end up here; see [Visitors and deliveries](visitors-and-deliveries.md).
