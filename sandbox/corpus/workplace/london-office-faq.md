# London office FAQ

The questions reception hears most often about Quill House. For the full picture, read the [London office guide](london-office.md). Maintained by Gwen Talbot; last reviewed 2026-10-12.

## Getting in

**When is the office open?**
From 07:00 to 21:00, Monday to Friday. Reception on floor 3 is staffed from 08:30 to 18:00. We are closed at weekends, on bank holidays and between Christmas and New Year.

**Can I come in at the weekend?**
Only for planned work, and only if Workplace has put you on the building's list. Email `workplace@quillmoor.example` at least two working days ahead with your name and the times.

**I have left my badge at home.**
Ask the building's security desk in the lobby for a day pass. They will check with reception, so expect a short wait before 08:30. The day pass does not open the bicycle store or release print jobs.

**I have lost my badge.**
Tell reception at once so that it can be switched off. You will usually have a new one the same day.

## Getting connected

**What is the Wi-Fi?**
Company laptops and phones join `QM-Staff` on their own; there is no password. Your personal phone goes on `QM-Guest`, whose passphrase changes daily and is shown at reception.

**My laptop will not join `QM-Staff`.**
Plug it into the network cable in any meeting room and restart it. If that does not fix it, post in `#workplace-london`.

**Where can I print?**
Two printers on each floor: by the galleys on floors 4 and 5, and in the post and print room on floor 3. Release your job with your badge.

## Desks and rooms

**Do I have to book a desk?**
Yes, in Perch, up to 14 days ahead, and check in by 10:00 or the booking is released. The details are in [Desk booking and hybrid working](desk-booking.md).

**Which days are busiest?**
Tuesdays and Thursdays, our team days. Book early for those, and expect the bicycle store and the Commons to be full.

**Where can I take a private call?**
In one of the phone booths: six on floor 4 and four each on floors 3 and 5. Booths are not bookable; please keep calls in them under an hour. For anything longer, book a room; see [Meeting rooms](meeting-rooms.md).

**Floor 5 looks different.**
Part of it is being refurbished. Read the [floor 5 refurbishment notice](floor-5-refurbishment.md) before booking a desk or room there.

## Getting here

**Is there car parking?**
No, not for staff. Building management has two accessible parking bays; if you need one, talk to Gwen Talbot and we will book it for you.

**Where do I leave my bicycle?**
In our caged section of the bicycle store on the lower ground floor, which holds 40 bicycle spaces. Showers, towels and lockers are next door. Private e-scooters are not allowed in the building.

**Can I charge my e-bike battery at my desk?**
No. Batteries may not be charged anywhere in Quill House.

## Day to day

**Is breakfast provided?**
On Tuesdays and Thursdays, in the Commons from 08:00 to 10:00. Fruit is out on the other days.

**Can I have parcels delivered to the office?**
Small ones, yes, addressed with your name and floor. Collect them from reception within two working days. See [Visitors and deliveries](visitors-and-deliveries.md) for large deliveries and the rules on personal parcels.

**Can I bring a guest?**
Yes, if you register them in Perch at least 24 hours ahead and stay with them. Children come in for family days only. Dogs are not allowed, except assistance dogs.

**I have lost something.**
Ask at reception, which keeps found items for 30 days. See [Lost property](lost-property.md).

**It is too cold on my side of the floor.**
Post in `#workplace-london` with the floor and the area. We pass it to building management, who adjust the zone; it can take an hour to feel the difference.

**Who are the first aiders?**
Their names and floors are in [First aiders and the wellbeing room](first-aiders.md), and on the board by each lift lobby.

**The fire alarm is ringing on a Wednesday morning.**
At 11:00, for under a minute, it is the weekly test. If it keeps ringing, leave the building. See [Fire safety and evacuation](fire-safety.md).

**I am visiting from Paris.**
Book a desk in Perch like anyone else, and reception will print you a badge on arrival. [Booking business travel](business-travel.md) covers the journey.
