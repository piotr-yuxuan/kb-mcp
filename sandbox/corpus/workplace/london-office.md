# London office guide

**Owner:** Gwen Talbot, workplace manager, London
**Contact:** `workplace@quillmoor.example`, or `#workplace-london` in chat
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-12

This is the guide to Quill House, where around 300 of us work on floors 3 to 5. It covers how to get in, what is on each floor, the Wi-Fi, cycling, food, accessibility and who to ask when something breaks. If you are after a quick answer, the [London office FAQ](london-office-faq.md) is shorter. For where to sit, see [Desk booking and hybrid working](desk-booking.md); for rooms, see [Meeting rooms](meeting-rooms.md).

> [!NOTE]
> Part of floor 5 is closed while it is refurbished. The [floor 5 refurbishment notice](floor-5-refurbishment.md) lists the desks and rooms affected and where the teams from that floor are sitting in the meantime.

## Getting in

Quill House is a shared building. The ground floor lobby, the lifts and the security desk belong to the building's management, and other companies occupy the floors below and above ours. Quillmoor has floors 3, 4 and 5 to itself.

The main entrance on the front of the building is step-free and is the one to use. A side entrance with a short flight of steps opens onto the cycle ramp and the lower ground floor; it is badge-only and locks at 19:00.

From the lobby, the speed gates take your Quillmoor badge. Four passenger lifts serve every floor; the goods lift at the back is for deliveries and is not for general use. The doors from the lift lobbies and the stairwells onto floors 3, 4 and 5 open only with a Quillmoor badge.

### Your badge

Reception on floor 3 prints your badge on your first day, with a photo taken at the desk. It opens the speed gates, our three floors, the bicycle store and the printers. Keep it with you when you move around the building: you need it to come back in from the stairs, the kitchen on another floor or the bicycle store.

- **Forgotten badge.** The building's security desk in the lobby gives you a day pass once floor 3 reception has confirmed who you are. A day pass opens the gates and our floors but not the bicycle store or the printers. Hand it back on your way out.
- **Lost badge.** Tell reception or `workplace@quillmoor.example` straight away. We switch the old badge off within the hour and print a new one, usually the same day. See [Lost property](lost-property.md) if you think you left it in the building.
- **Broken badge.** Bring it to reception for a swap.
- **First day.** Reception will have your badge ready. Everything else about your first fortnight, from your buddy to your laptop, is in [New joiner onboarding](../people/onboarding.md).

## Opening hours

The office is open from 07:00 to 21:00, Monday to Friday. Badges open the speed gates from 07:00. At 21:00 building security walk our floors, and the lighting and air handling switch to night mode at 21:15. If you are still at your desk at 21:00, you will be asked to pack up.

Reception on floor 3 is staffed from 08:30 to 18:00. Outside those hours, visitors cannot be signed in, deliveries are not accepted and the building's security desk in the lobby is your first port of call.

The office is closed at weekends and on bank holidays, and between Christmas and New Year. We announce the exact closure dates in chat every November.

### Working outside office hours

Planned work outside office hours, such as a weekend release or a quarter-end close, needs a request to `workplace@quillmoor.example` at least two working days ahead, with the names of everyone coming in and the times. We send the list to building security, who will not let anyone up who is not on it.

Out-of-hours incidents are handled from home, as described in [the on-call rota and paging policy](../incidents/on-call.md). If someone really must come into the building during an incident, phone the building security desk: its number is printed on the back of your badge.

## Floor by floor

### Lower ground floor

The bicycle store, the changing rooms and the drying room. Our part of the bicycle store is the caged section on the left of the ramp, opened by badge. The changing rooms have eight showers, one of them in an accessible room of its own, as well as hairdryers and an ironing board. Towels are put out from Monday to Friday.

### Ground floor

The building lobby and the building's security desk, staffed around the clock; the lifts; and, at the rear, the service yard and the loading bay. Nothing on the ground floor is Quillmoor's own, so please do not leave parcels, bicycles or boxes there. Deliveries are covered in [Visitors and deliveries](visitors-and-deliveries.md).

### Floor 3: reception, the Commons and the business teams

You come out of the lifts facing Quillmoor reception. Visitors wait on the sofas to the right, and the four rooms we use for guests are around the corner: Foolscap, Margin, Serif and Graphite.

Behind reception is the Commons, our main kitchen and the space where we hold all-hands meetings, talks and socials. It seats around 150 for an all-hands and has the best coffee machines in the building.

The rest of the floor belongs to People, Finance, Legal, Merchant Support and Workplace. The wellbeing room is next to the Commons, beside the accessible toilet; see [First aiders and the wellbeing room](first-aiders.md). The post and print room is behind reception, with the stationery cupboard and the returns shelf.

### Floor 4: engineering

Most of engineering sits on floor 4: Payments Core, Payouts, Ledger, Platform, Site Reliability and Security, in neighbourhoods signed at the end of each row. The north end of the floor is the quiet zone: no calls, no meetings at desks and no music without headphones.

Floor 4 has four meeting rooms (Carbon, Sepia, Indigo and Umber) and six phone booths along the east wall. Sepia is the incident room: anyone can book it, but an incident always takes priority. The galley by the lifts has tea, coffee, a fridge and a water tap.

### Floor 5: product, data, design and machine learning

Floor 5 houses Data Platform, Risk ML, Design System, Merchant Experience and Product. The boardroom, Folio, is at the west end, along with Vermilion; Quarto and Octavo are on the east side. Floor 5 has its own galley and four phone booths.

While the floor is being refurbished, some of these rooms and desks are out of use. Check [the refurbishment notice](floor-5-refurbishment.md) before you book anything on floor 5.

## Wi-Fi and printing

There are two Wi-Fi networks across our three floors.

- **`QM-Staff`** is for company laptops and company phones. They join automatically using the certificate installed when the device was set up, so there is no password to type. If your laptop will not connect, restart it while on the office network cable in any meeting room, and if that does not help, post in `#workplace-london`.
- **`QM-Guest`** is for visitors and for your personal phone or tablet. It reaches the internet only, not our internal services, and it is slower. The passphrase changes every day; it is shown on the screen at reception and printed on visitor badges. Do not share it outside the building.

Every meeting room also has a network cable and a display cable at the table, for when wireless screen sharing lets you down.

Each floor has two printers: on floors 4 and 5 they stand by the galleys, and on floor 3 they are in the post and print room. Send your job from your laptop and release it by tapping your badge on the printer. Jobs you do not release are deleted after 24 hours. Printing is black and white unless you choose colour.

## Cycling, showers and lockers

Our bicycle store holds 40 bicycle spaces: 32 on two-tier racks, six floor stands for heavier bicycles and two wider bays for cargo or adapted cycles. Spaces are first come, first served. On Tuesdays and Thursdays the store is usually full by about 09:15, so if you cycle on team days, come in early or have a back-up plan.

A few house rules keep it working:

- Lock your bicycle to the rack, not to another bicycle or the cage.
- Bicycles should not stay overnight unless you are away on business; leave a note on the saddle with your name and return date.
- Bicycles left for more than a week without a note are tagged, and removed a month after tagging.
- Privately owned e-scooters are not allowed in the building, and e-bike batteries may not be charged anywhere inside it.
- Folding bicycles can come up to your floor; store them under your desk or in the cupboard by the lifts.

The cycle-to-work scheme is described in [Benefits in the UK](../people/benefits-uk.md).

Each floor has a bank of day lockers by the lifts; the lower ground floor has more for cyclists. Day lockers are opened and emptied every Friday at 21:00, and anything left in them goes to [lost property](lost-property.md). If you cycle in most days and need a locker for the week, ask reception for a weekly one.

## Food and drink

The Commons on floor 3 has two coffee machines, a hot and filtered water tap, three fridges, microwaves and two dishwashers. Floors 4 and 5 have smaller galleys with tea, coffee, milk and a fridge.

On team days, Tuesday and Thursday, breakfast is laid out in the Commons from 08:00 to 10:00: bread, cereals, yoghurt, fruit, and something hot on Thursdays. Fruit is out on every other day. Every item is labelled with its allergens; if you have a dietary need the breakfast does not cover, tell `workplace@quillmoor.example` and we will sort something out.

The fridges are cleared every Friday at 16:00. Anything unlabelled goes, as does anything past its date. Please load the dishwasher rather than leaving mugs in the sink: the cleaners empty dishwashers but do not wash up.

Once a month, on a Thursday from 17:30, the Commons hosts drinks for whoever is in. There are always soft drinks and alcohol-free options, and the evening ends well before 21:00.

## Accessibility

- Step-free access from the street through the main entrance, to every floor by lift. Lifts announce each floor.
- Accessible toilets on every floor, next to the lift lobbies.
- Hearing loops at reception and in Foolscap and Folio.
- Sit-stand desks in every neighbourhood, and a small number of desks set aside for colleagues with an agreed adjustment (see [Desk booking and hybrid working](desk-booking.md)).
- A personal evacuation plan for anyone who would need help to leave the building in an emergency; see [Fire safety and evacuation](fire-safety.md).

If something about the building makes your working day harder, tell Gwen Talbot directly. You do not need to explain why.

## Keeping the office pleasant

With around 300 people across three floors, a few habits go a long way.

- **Take calls in a booth or a room**, not at your desk. Headsets are fine for a quick reply, not for an hour-long meeting.
- **Respect the quiet zone** at the north end of floor 4.
- **Leave your desk clear** at the end of the day. Someone else will sit there tomorrow; use a locker for anything you leave overnight.
- **Eat in the kitchens** where you can. A sandwich at your desk is fine; a hot lunch with a strong smell is not.
- **Sort your rubbish.** Each galley has bins for food waste, mixed recycling, glass and general waste. Batteries and small electrical items go in the box in the post and print room.
- **No pets.** Assistance dogs are welcome; tell reception so that we can put down a water bowl.

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|

| Too hot, too cold or stuffy | Post in `#workplace-london`; we ask building management to adjust the zone |
| Wi-Fi or printer fault | Post in `#workplace-london` |
| Leak, flood, smell of burning, someone stuck in a lift | Phone the building security desk at once, then tell reception |
| Something spilt in a kitchen | Clean up what you can and tell reception; cleaners are on site until 14:00 |
| Anything that feels unsafe | Tell reception or Gwen Talbot the same day |
