# Booking business travel

Owner: Gwen Talbot, workplace manager, with the Finance team. Last reviewed 2026-08-19.

The Workplace team books business travel for everyone at Quillmoor, in London, in Paris and working remotely. We book through the company's travel account, so tickets and hotel rooms are paid centrally and charged to your cost centre: you do not pay for them yourself or claim them back.

This page covers how to book. What you may spend, what you can claim and the daily allowances are set by Finance, in the [Expenses policy](../finance/expenses.md), the [Notes de frais (France)](../finance/notes-de-frais.md) and [Travel per diems](../finance/per-diems.md).

## Before you book

Ask yourself whether the trip is needed. A good video call replaces many short visits, and a half-day meeting rarely justifies a return flight.

Then get your manager's approval in writing. An email or a chat message is enough, as long as it names the trip and the dates.

## How to book

Email `workplace@quillmoor.example` with the subject "Travel request" and:

- your full name exactly as it appears on your passport or identity card;
- where you are going, the dates and the times that suit you;
- the purpose of the trip, in a sentence;
- your cost centre (see the [cost centre list](../finance/cost-centres.md) if you are not sure);
- your manager's approval, forwarded or pasted in;
- whether you need a hotel, and any accessibility or dietary needs for the journey.

Workplace replies with options within one working day. Once you confirm, we book, and the tickets and itinerary arrive by email.

### How much notice

| Trip | Ask at least |
|---|---|
| A day trip by rail within the UK | 5 working days ahead |
| London to Paris, or Paris to London | 10 working days ahead |
| Anywhere else abroad | 15 working days ahead |

We can book at shorter notice, but fares rise quickly, so tell us why it is urgent and we will do what we can.

## How we travel

- **Between London and Paris**, the cross-Channel train is the default. It runs from city centre to city centre, and you can work on the way. We book a flight only when the train genuinely does not work for the trip.
- **Within the UK**, rail comes first. A flight within the UK needs a reason, such as a journey of more than five hours by train.
- **Class of travel and hotel limits** are set in the expenses policy linked above; Workplace books within them.
- **Hotels.** Workplace keeps a short list of hotels near each office, with agreed rates. We book one of them unless you have a good reason to stay elsewhere. The room is billed to the company; anything else you order at the hotel is yours to pay and, where the expenses policy allows, to claim.

## Visiting the other office

Most of our travel is between Quill House and Maison Lanterne. A few things make the visit smoother.

- **Book a desk in Perch** at the other office, under the usual rules: see [Desk booking and hybrid working](desk-booking.md). Team days are Tuesday and Thursday in both offices, so they make good days to visit.
- **Your badge** does not open the other building. Mention the dates in your travel request and the other office's reception will have a temporary badge ready when you arrive.
- **Bank holidays differ.** Quill House closes on UK bank holidays and Maison Lanterne on French public holidays, so check before you choose your dates. The [London office guide](london-office.md) and the [Paris office guide](bureau-de-paris.md) have the details.
- **Wi-Fi.** Your company laptop joins `QM-Staff` on its own in both offices.

## Devices, data and longer stays

Before you take a laptop or phone abroad, read [Travelling with company devices](../security/travel-with-devices.md).

If you want to stay on and work from another country for a while, that is not business travel; see [Working from abroad](../people/working-abroad.md). If you add a few personal days to a business trip, agree them with your manager, book the leave in Haven as usual, and pay any extra cost yourself, such as additional hotel nights or a later return train.

## Insurance and emergencies

Quillmoor's business travel insurance covers every approved trip, including medical emergencies, cancellations and lost luggage. Finance holds the certificate and the claims details; ask `finance@quillmoor.example` before you travel if you need a copy.

Your itinerary includes the 24-hour assistance line of our travel account. Call it for missed connections, cancellations and rebooking outside office hours: it can change your tickets and hotel without involving us.

If something serious happens while you are away, such as an accident, an illness or a security incident at your destination, get yourself safe first, then tell your manager and Gwen Talbot (or Élodie Vasseur in Paris) as soon as you can.

## Changes and cancellations

Tell `workplace@quillmoor.example` the moment your plans change. Hotel nights we book can usually be cancelled free of charge until 12:00 the day before arrival; after that, the night is normally charged. For non-refundable tickets, we will try to keep the value as a credit for a later trip.

If you have to book something yourself, for example because your train was cancelled late at night and the assistance line could not help, keep every receipt and claim it in Tally as the expenses policy describes.

## Travel for guests

Workplace can also book travel for people we invite: candidates coming for an interview, merchants coming to a workshop, or a contractor coming to work with a team. Send the same details, with the cost centre that pays, and remember to register them in Perch as visitors too; see [Visitors and deliveries](visitors-and-deliveries.md).
