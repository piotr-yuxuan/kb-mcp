# Office notice: floor 5 refurbishment

*Posted by Gwen Talbot on 2026-08-24. Updated on 2026-10-14, when phase 2 began.*

Floor 5 of Quill House is being refurbished in two phases, so that half of the floor stays in use throughout. This notice says what is closed, where the floor 5 teams are sitting, and what to expect in the meantime.

## Where we are

| Phase | Area | Dates | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | West side of floor 5 | 14 September to 9 October 2026 | Finished |
| 2 | East side of floor 5 | From 12 October 2026, for about six weeks | Under way |

We will post the reopening date for the east side here and in `#workplace-london` as soon as the contractor confirms it.

## What is closed during phase 2

- **The east side of floor 5**, behind the hoarding from the lift lobby to the east wall.
- **40 desks.** They are hidden in Perch until the works end.
- **Quarto and Octavo**, the two meeting rooms on the east side. Existing bookings in them have been cancelled and their organisers told. Folio and Vermilion, on the west side, stay open.
- **The floor 5 galley**, which is being rebuilt. Use the galley on floor 4 or the Commons on floor 3.

The lifts, the toilets, the four phone booths on the west side and both stairs stay open. The works do not change any fire escape route; see [Fire safety and evacuation](fire-safety.md).

## Where the floor 5 teams are sitting

| Team | During phase 2 |
|---|---|
| Data Platform | West side of floor 5 (its usual place) |
| Risk ML | West side of floor 5 (its usual place) |
| Product | West side of floor 5 |
| Design System | Temporary neighbourhood at the west end of floor 3 |
| Merchant Experience | Temporary neighbourhood at the west end of floor 3 |

The temporary neighbourhood on floor 3 is set up in Perch like any other: book it as usual, within the normal rules in [Desk booking and hybrid working](desk-booking.md). Floor 3 is busy on team days, so book early.

## Noise and dust

The contractor does drilling and other noisy work only before 09:00 and after 18:00 on weekdays, and on Saturdays, when the office is closed. Quieter work, such as painting, cabling and fitting furniture, goes on during the day.

If the noise is disturbing you during the day, post in `#workplace-london` with the time and where you are sitting. Gwen will take it up with the site manager the same day.

The hoarding is sealed and the air on the east side is extracted separately, so dust should not reach the working areas. If you notice dust, a smell of paint or solvents, or a headache you think is linked to the works, tell reception.

## Contractors on the floor

Contractors sign in at the building's security desk, use the goods lift and the loading bay, and wear a contractor's badge at all times. They should never be on the working side of the hoarding without a Workplace escort. If you see one who seems lost, point them to reception.

## What you get at the end

- Sit-stand desks throughout floor 5.
- Two new focus rooms for one or two people, bookable in Perch.
- Four more phone booths.
- A larger galley, with a second fridge and a dishwasher.
- Quarto and Octavo refitted, with new screens and cameras.
- Better ventilation and lighting on both sides of the floor.

The number of desks on floor 5 stays at 80.
