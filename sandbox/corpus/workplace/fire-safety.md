# Fire safety and evacuation

Owners: Gwen Talbot (London) and Élodie Vasseur (Paris). Last reviewed 2026-09-30.

Read this once when you join, and again if you move floor or office. It takes three minutes.

## If you discover a fire

1. Raise the alarm at the nearest red call point. There is one beside every stair door.
2. Call the emergency services: 999 in London, 112 in Paris.
3. Leave the building. Use an extinguisher only if you have been trained and the fire is small enough to be safe.

## When the alarm sounds in Quill House

| What you hear | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A continuous alarm | Evacuate | Leave at once by the nearest stair |
| An intermittent tone | An alarm elsewhere in the building | Stop work, get ready to leave and wait for your floor warden |
| A short ring at 11:00 on a Wednesday | The weekly test | Nothing, unless it carries on for more than a minute |

When you evacuate:

- Use the north or south stair, whichever is closer. Never use the lifts.
- Do not go back for your bag, laptop or coat.
- Close doors behind you, but do not lock them.
- Go to the assembly point on the pedestrian square in front of Quill House, and stand by the sign with your floor number.
- Do not go back in until a fire warden or building security says so.

## When the alarm sounds in Maison Lanterne

The building has a single alarm tone: when you hear it, leave. It is tested on the first Monday of each month at 10:00.

Leave by the main stair or by the emergency stair at the end of the west wing, never by the lifts, and gather on the square across the street from the main entrance. The building's own team decides when you can go back in.

## Fire wardens

In London, each floor has two fire wardens on duty every day, in orange high-visibility vests. Their names are on the board in each lift lobby. When the alarm sounds, they sweep the floor, including the toilets, phone booths, meeting rooms and the wellbeing room, and then take the roll call at the assembly point.

In Paris, the same job is done by the *guides-files*, who lead the way out, and the *serre-files*, who leave last and check that nobody is left behind. Their names are on the board in l'Atelier.

The roll call uses the day's list from Perch: every desk check-in and every registered visitor. This is one more reason to check in at your desk and to register your visitors (see [Desk booking and hybrid working](desk-booking.md) and [Visitors and deliveries](visitors-and-deliveries.md)).

We always need more wardens, especially for Mondays and Fridays. The training takes half a day and is refreshed every year. If you are interested, tell Gwen Talbot or Élodie Vasseur.

## If you would need help to leave

If you would find it hard to use the stairs quickly, for any reason, ask Gwen or Élodie for a personal evacuation plan. It sets out how you will leave, who will help and where you will wait. Plans are reviewed every year, and sooner if your needs change. A temporary reason, such as a broken ankle or late pregnancy, counts too.

In Quill House, every stair landing on floors 3 to 5 is a refuge with an intercom to the building's security desk, and there is an evacuation chair at the top of each stair. Wardens are trained to use them.

## Visitors and contractors

If you are hosting visitors, take them with you to the assembly point and tell a warden they are there. Contractors working on the [floor 5 refurbishment](floor-5-refurbishment.md) follow their own supervisor, who reports to our wardens at the assembly point. The works do not change any escape route.

## Drills

Quill House holds two evacuation drills a year, one announced and one not. The most recent, on 24 September 2026, cleared our three floors in 6 minutes 40 seconds. Maison Lanterne's drills are run by the building, also twice a year.

## Preventing fires

- Do not charge e-bike or e-scooter batteries anywhere in either building.
- No personal heaters, candles, incense or extension leads plugged into other extension leads.
- Keep fire doors shut and corridors, stairs and exits clear of boxes and bicycles.
- Report damaged cables, scorched sockets or anything that smells of burning to reception straight away.

For injuries and first aid, see [First aiders and the wellbeing room](first-aiders.md).
