## Recording an accident

Any injury at work, however small, is written in the accident book: at reception on floor 3 in London, and with Élodie in Paris. The first aider who helped you fills it in with you. Workplace reviews the book every month, looking for a loose carpet tile or a sharp corner before it hurts somebody else.

## The wellbeing room

### London

The wellbeing room is on floor 3, next to the Commons and beside the accessible toilet. It has a day bed, an armchair, dimmable lighting, a sink and a small fridge. The lock on the door shows "engaged" on the outside.

Use it if you:

- feel unwell and need to lie down,
- need to take medication in private,
- want somewhere to pray or sit quietly,
- are nursing or expressing milk (the fridge is for labelled expressed milk only),
- need a few quiet minutes after a difficult call.

It is not for phone calls, meetings or a nap after lunch.

The room cannot be booked in Perch, so that nobody's use of it appears in a calendar. If you need it regularly, for example while you are nursing, tell Gwen Talbot, who will arrange a time privately. If you are in there for more than an hour because you feel unwell, tell reception so that someone can check on you.

### Paris

In Maison Lanterne, the *salle de repos* in the west wing serves the same purpose, on the same terms. It is smaller, with an armchair and a fridge. Élodie Vasseur arranges regular use.

## Becoming a first aider

We would like at least two first aiders in each office on every working day, and we are short on Mondays and Fridays. The training is a three-day course during working hours, paid for by Quillmoor, with a half-day refresher every year and a full requalification every three years. Talk to Gwen Talbot or Élodie Vasseur if you would like to volunteer.
