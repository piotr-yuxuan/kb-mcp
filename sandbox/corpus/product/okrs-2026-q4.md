**Quarter:** October to December 2026, from ENG Sprint 39 (28 September). **Owner:** Ama Rowntree. **Published:** 25 September 2026. **Status updated:** 19 October 2026, at the ENG Sprint 40 review. **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-19.

This quarter finishes what Q3 started. Two epics, ENG-26 and ENG-30, reach the end of their planned span in ENG Sprint 40 with about six in ten of their tickets done; the teams have agreed to extend both for the rest of the quarter rather than split them. We start almost nothing new, because the change freezes on the [change freeze calendar](../release/change-freeze-2026.md) leave few launch days after mid-November.

Confidence is one of **on track**, **at risk** or **off track**, as in the [quarterly planning process](quarterly-planning.md). C marks a committed key result, A an aspirational one.
