This is the internal summary of our standard prices, for anyone who needs to answer "what does it cost?" without opening a contract. The contract signed with a merchant always wins over this page. Prices exclude VAT where it applies.

## Card acceptance: Quillmoor Pay

Charged per successful payment. Declined and failed payments are free.

| Card | Fee |
|---|---|
| UK consumer cards | 1.4% + 20p |
| UK business cards | 2.1% + 20p |
| European consumer cards | 1.9% + 20p (or + €0.20 for payments in euros) |
| Cards issued elsewhere | 2.9% + 20p |

Merchants processing more than £5 million a month can ask for volume pricing, either lower blended rates or an interchange-plus arrangement in which the card schemes' costs are passed through and our margin is shown separately. The commercial team prepares these with finance.

## Payouts: Quillmoor Payouts

| Payout | Fee |
|---|---|
| Pounds to a UK bank account | 10p per payout |
| Euros through SEPA | €0.15 per payout |
| Currency conversion, where a merchant settles or pays out in a currency other than the payment's | 1.0% above the reference rate |

Payouts are not charged when they fail and are returned. Merchants who settle euros in euros pay no conversion on those payments; see the [multi-currency brief](brief-multi-currency.md).

## Refunds and disputes

- **Refunds:** no fee to issue a refund, whether through support or self-serve. The fee on the original payment is not returned.
- **Disputes and chargebacks:** a dispute fee applies to each chargeback. The amount and when it is refunded are on [Chargebacks and disputes](../support/chargebacks.md).

## Included at no charge

- Merchant Hub, for any number of users.
- The Merchant API, including webhooks and the sandbox merchant accounts.
- Settlement reports and exports.
- Standard support, with the priorities and response times on [Ticket priorities and SLAs](../support/priorities-and-slas.md).

## Not yet priced

- **Instant payouts.** In discovery; a working assumption for the fee is on the [instant payouts brief](brief-instant-payouts.md). Do not quote it to merchants.

## Strategic merchants and exceptions

Strategic merchants have individual pricing agreed at contract; the list and how they are served is on [Strategic merchants](../support/strategic-merchants.md).

Any discount below the volume bands above needs approval from the head of product and the chief financial officer before it is offered. Record the approval in the contract request in Clausebook.

## Changing prices

Standard prices are reviewed once a year, in the Q1 planning round, with finance. A change for existing merchants follows the notice period in their MSA; see the [MSA clause guide](../legal/msa-clause-guide.md). New prices go on this page and in the merchant-facing price list on the same day.
