*Owner: Saoirse Lindqvist, general counsel. Approved by the audit committee. Last reviewed
2026-09-10.*

If you see wrongdoing at Quillmoor, we want to hear about it, and we will protect you for
telling us. This page explains what to report, where, and what happens next. It applies to
employees, contractors, agency workers, former staff and job applicants, in the UK and in
France.

## What to report

Report anything you reasonably believe shows, or may show, that someone at Quillmoor or
acting for us has done, is doing or is about to do something seriously wrong. For example:

- fraud, theft, bribery or money laundering;
- breaches of the law or of the card schemes' rules, including on data protection;
- a danger to someone's health or safety;
- harm to merchants or their customers, such as misleading them about their funds;
- deliberately hiding any of the above.

You do not need proof, only a reasonable belief. You are not expected to investigate
yourself, and you should not.

Whistleblowing is not the route for a personal complaint about your own pay, workload or
treatment by a manager. Those are grievances, handled by People at
`people@quillmoor.example`. If you are not sure which it is, report it here; we will
redirect it if needed and tell you.

## Where to report

Choose whichever channel you are most comfortable with:

| Channel | How | Anonymous? |
|---|---|---|
| The general counsel | Saoirse Lindqvist, in person or at `legal@quillmoor.example` | No |
| The whistleblowing address | `whistleblowing@quillmoor.example`, read only by the general counsel and the chair of the audit committee | No |
| The independent reporting line | `https://speakup.example.com/quillmoor`, or the phone number on that page, in English or French, at any hour | Yes, if you wish |
| The chair of the audit committee | Through the reporting line, choosing "Board only" | Yes, if you wish |

Use the "Board only" option if your concern involves the general counsel, the chief
executive or another member of the executive team.

The independent line is run by an outside provider. It passes reports to us without your
name unless you choose to give it, and lets us ask you questions through the same line
while you stay anonymous.

You may also report to the relevant regulator directly. You do not have to report
internally first, although we hope you will.

## What happens next

1. **Acknowledgement within 7 days** of your report, through the channel you used.
2. **Assessment.** The general counsel, or the chair of the audit committee if the report
   involves the executive team, decides how to investigate and who should do it. Nobody
   named in a report takes part in handling it.
3. **Investigation.** This may be internal or carried out by an outside investigator. We
   may ask you for more information.
4. **Feedback within 3 months** of the acknowledgement, telling you what we did, as far as
   we can without breaching others' confidentiality.

Reports and investigation files are kept confidential and separate from personnel files.
Your identity is shared only with those who need it to investigate, and only with your
consent unless the law requires otherwise.

## Protection

Nobody will be dismissed, disciplined, passed over or treated worse for raising a concern
in good faith, even if it turns out to be mistaken. Retaliating against a whistleblower,
or trying to find out who made an anonymous report, is gross misconduct.

If you feel you have been treated badly because you raised a concern, tell the general
counsel or use the reporting line. Support is also available through the services listed
in [Wellbeing support](../people/wellbeing.md).

Knowingly making a false report to harm someone is itself a disciplinary matter. That is
very rare, and it is not a reason to hold back a genuine concern.
