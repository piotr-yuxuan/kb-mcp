> [!IMPORTANT]
> Never let a merchant, partner or supplier imply that Quillmoor endorses them without
> written approval from Legal and Product. Ask first, publish later.

**Owners:** Legal (trade marks and permissions) and Product (brand). Last reviewed
2026-05-12.

"Quillmoor", "Quillmoor Pay", "Quillmoor Payouts" and the quill logo are registered trade
marks in the UK and across Europe. "Merchant Hub" is not registered; treat it as a
product name, not a brand of its own. Our marks are only worth something if we use them
consistently and stop others from using them loosely, which is what this page is for.

## Inside Quillmoor

You may use the name and logo freely in internal documents, slides and the wiki. Take the
logo files from the brand portal at `https://brand.quillmoor.example`, never from a
screenshot or a web search, and use the colour and spacing rules in
[Inkwell design tokens and principles](../design/tokens.md). For how to write the product
names, follow the [content style guide](../design/content-style.md).

The essentials:

- Write "Quillmoor" with a capital Q and nothing else capitalised. Never "QuillMoor",
  "QUILLMOOR" in running text, or "QM" in anything a merchant will see.
- Do not stretch, recolour, outline or animate the logo, or put it on a busy background.
- Do not combine the logo with another mark to make a new one, such as a team logo.
  Internal team names may have their own emoji in chat; they may not have their own logo.

## Merchants

The MSA allows merchants to display the "Powered by Quillmoor" badge at checkout and to
name us as their payment provider in their terms and privacy notice. That is all it
allows. A merchant that wants to do more needs written permission:

| A merchant wants to | What to do |
|---|---|
| Show the "Powered by Quillmoor" badge at checkout | Nothing; the MSA covers it |
| Name Quillmoor in its terms or privacy notice | Nothing; the MSA covers it |
| Announce the partnership in a press release or blog post | Raise a request in Clausebook; Legal and Product approve the text |
| Use our logo in its marketing or a case study | Raise a request; we usually ask for a review of the final design |
| Use a Quillmoor product name in its own product or domain name | Refuse |

## Using other companies' names

The same rules work in reverse. Do not put a merchant's or partner's logo in a public
slide, on our website or in a case study without its written permission, even if it is a
happy customer. Permission is recorded in Clausebook against the merchant, so that the
next person to ask can check.

## Domains and social accounts

Only Legal registers domains containing "quillmoor" or a product name, so that renewals
are not missed. Do not open social media accounts in Quillmoor's name; talk to Product if
your team needs a public presence.

## If you see misuse

If a merchant, a former partner or a stranger is using our name in a misleading way, for
example a website pretending to be Merchant Hub, send the link to
`legal@quillmoor.example`. If it looks like an attempt to collect merchants' credentials,
also report it to Security at `phish@quillmoor.example`.
