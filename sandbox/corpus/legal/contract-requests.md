title: "Requesting a contract review"

*Owner: Ruairi Fenn, legal operations manager. Last reviewed 2026-08-19.*

Every request for Legal's help with a contract goes through Clausebook. Requests sent by
email or chat are not lost, but they are not tracked either, and they wait until someone
copies them into Clausebook. Start there and you save a day.

## Before you raise a request

Check whether you need Legal at all. You can send and sign these yourself:

- our mutual or one-way NDA, unchanged (see [Mutual NDA: template and guidance](nda.md));
- a merchant MSA and order form on the current templates, with no special terms;
- any other template in the [contract template library](template-library.md) marked
  self-serve, unchanged.

Everything else needs a request.

## Request types

| Type | Use it for | Target first response |
|---|---|---|
| NDA (self-serve) | Generating and signing our NDA template | Immediate, no review |
| Third-party NDA review | A counterparty's NDA | 2 business days |
| MSA deviation | A merchant's changes to the MSA or order form | 5 business days |
| Supplier contract | Anything Quillmoor buys, on our paper or theirs | 5 business days |
| Data protection | DPAs, privacy questionnaires, new sub-processors | 5 business days, with the DPO |
| Partnership or other | Partnerships, letters of intent, anything unusual | Agreed at triage |

The target is for a first substantive response: comments, a mark-up or a clear answer. A
long negotiation takes longer, and the request stays open until the contract is signed.

## What to include

A request with everything below is usually answered within its target. A request missing
the counterparty's document or the value goes back to you.

1. The counterparty's full legal name and country.
2. The document, in an editable format. Scans and locked files slow us down.
3. The total contract value and how you calculated it, as defined in
   [Contract signature authority](contract-signature-authority.md).
4. What the contract is for, in two or three sentences, and what matters most to you
   commercially.
5. Whether personal data, cardholder data or source code will change hands.
6. Any deadline, with the reason for it.
7. For purchases: the approved purchase order in Tally.

## Urgent requests

Mark a request urgent only when a real business consequence follows from missing the date:
a go-live, a regulatory deadline, a renewal that would otherwise lock us in. Urgent
requests are triaged within 2 business hours. "The counterparty is chasing" is not, on
its own, a reason.

## What happens next

Legal operations triages new requests every business day at 10:00 and 15:00 UK time. At
triage a request is assigned to a lawyer, or returned to you if something is missing. You
can follow its status in Clausebook, and every comment from Legal appears on the request,
not in your inbox.

When the text is agreed, Legal locks the final version and Clausebook routes it for
signature. You do not need to send it to the signatory yourself.

## Tips from the Legal team

- Bring us in before the counterparty's lawyers send a mark-up, not after. A short call at
  the start avoids three rounds of comments later.
- Do not promise a counterparty a position you have not checked. "Let me confirm with our
  Legal team" costs nothing.
- One request per contract. If a supplier sends an NDA, a master agreement and an order
  form, raise one supplier contract request and attach all three.
