*Owner: Saoirse Lindqvist, general counsel. Last reviewed 2026-01-22.*

Merchants, suppliers and partners will offer you gifts and invitations, and you will want
to offer them some too. Most of this is ordinary courtesy and perfectly fine. The rules on
this page exist so that nobody, inside or outside Quillmoor, could reasonably think that a
gift bought a decision. They apply to everyone who works for Quillmoor, including
contractors, in the UK and in France.

## The short version

- **Register gifts over £50**, given or received, within 5 business days.
- Never give or accept cash, vouchers, gift cards or anything that can be turned into cash,
  whatever the amount.
- Never give or accept anything while a tender, contract negotiation or dispute with that
  party is open.
- Anything involving a public official needs Legal's approval first, at any value.
- If you are unsure, ask before you accept, not after.

## Gifts

A gift worth £50 or less, such as a branded notebook, a bottle of wine at the end of the
year or a box of chocolates for the team, needs no registration. Above that, register it,
including the giver, the occasion and an honest estimate of its value. Where the value is
unknown, estimate what it would cost to buy.

Count gifts from the same person or company over 12 months together. Three bottles of
wine at £20 each from the same supplier over a year need registering.

We may ask you to return a gift, share it with the team, or give it to the charity raffle at
the end of the year. That is not a criticism; it is how we keep the register meaningful.

## Hospitality

Hospitality means meals, drinks, events and travel. A working lunch or a dinner with a
merchant you are meeting anyway is normal business. Register hospitality over £50 a head,
and ask your manager first for:

- any event where the business content is thin, such as sporting or cultural events;
- anything involving travel or an overnight stay, which we pay for ourselves even if the
  host offers;
- anything to which a partner or family member is also invited.

When we are the host, the same thresholds apply, and the cost is claimed under the
[expenses policy](../finance/expenses.md) with the names of the guests.

## How to register

Use the form at `https://legal.quillmoor.example/gifts`. It takes two minutes and asks for:

1. given or received;
2. the other party and the person involved;
3. what it was, the date, and its estimated value in pounds or euros;
4. whether any decision involving that party is pending.

Legal reviews the register every quarter and reports a summary to the audit committee.

## Examples

| Situation | Register? | Allowed? |
|---|---|---|
| A supplier sends a £35 hamper at the end of the year | No | Yes |
| A merchant invites you to a £120-a-head dinner after a quarterly review | Yes | Yes, with your manager's agreement |
| A supplier bidding for our observability contract offers concert tickets | Yes | No, the bid is open |
| A partner offers a £100 gift card to thank you for a referral | Yes | No, it can be turned into cash |
| You want to give a regulator's staff branded umbrellas after a visit | Yes | Only with Legal's approval first |

These rules sit under the [anti-bribery policy](anti-bribery.md), which explains why they
matter and what to do if something feels wrong.
