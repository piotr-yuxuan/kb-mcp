The library holds every contract template that Quillmoor uses, in Clausebook. This page
lists what is there, which templates you may use without Legal, and the rules for using
them. Legal operations maintains the library; the general counsel approves every new
template and every new version.

Last reviewed 2026-06-30 by Ruairi Fenn.

## The rule that matters most

Always generate a contract from Clausebook. Never reuse a signed contract as a starting
point, and never edit a copy saved on your laptop. Clausebook always serves the current
version, fills in the counterparty's details and records which version was signed. A copy
from last year may contain clauses we have since withdrawn.

"Self-serve" means you may send the template without a Legal review, provided you change
nothing but the open fields. It does not change who signs: that is set by
[Contract signature authority](contract-signature-authority.md).

Employment contracts and offer letters are not in this library. People keeps them in
Haven.

## Open fields and locked text

Each template has open fields, highlighted in Clausebook, for names, addresses, dates,
prices and the purpose. Everything else is locked. If a counterparty asks to change locked
text, the contract stops being self-serve: raise a request as described in
[Requesting a contract review](contract-requests.md), and say which template it is.

## Which template for a merchant?

Use the English-law MSA by default. Offer the French-law MSA to merchants incorporated in
France, or when the merchant asks for French. The commercial terms are the same in both;
clause guidance is in the [MSA clause guide](msa-clause-guide.md).

## Asking for a new template

If your team signs the same kind of contract more than five times a year, ask for a
template. Raise a "Partnership or other" request describing the contract, attach two or
three examples you have signed, and say how many you expect to sign. Legal drafts it, the
general counsel approves it, and it appears here.
