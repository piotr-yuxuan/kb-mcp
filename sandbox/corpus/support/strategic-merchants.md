title: "Strategic merchants"

Owner: Femi Haldane, head of customer support. Last reviewed 2026-07-29.

A small number of marketplaces account for a large share of what Quillmoor processes. We call them strategic merchants. They get a named owner in support and closer contact; they do not get different rules.

## Who is strategic

Strategic status is agreed each quarter by Femi Haldane and Ama Rowntree, head of product, from processing volume, contract terms and how much the merchant depends on Quillmoor Payouts. It is recorded on the merchant's account in Harbour with the tag `strategic`, and the Harbour view **Strategic merchants** lists them. In July 2026 the list held 14 merchants, 3 of them French-speaking.

Do not tell a merchant that they are, or are not, on the list. Merchants know they have a named contact; the label is internal.

## How their tickets are handled

| What | For strategic merchants |
|---|---|
| Routing | Every ticket goes to the `strategic` queue, whatever its subject |
| Owner | A named T2 owner and a deputy, shown on the account in Harbour |
| Priority | The same P1 to P4 scale as every merchant; see [Ticket priorities and SLAs](priorities-and-slas.md) |
| Contact | For P1 and P2, the owner also phones the merchant's named contact, in addition to the written reply |
| Reviews | A monthly service call with the owner; a quarterly review with Femi Haldane, the merchant and, when useful, the engineering team that owns the services they use |
| Out of hours | P1 tickets follow the usual route through the support duty agent and the payments on-call; the owner is told the next morning |

If a strategic merchant's ticket arrives in another queue, move it to `strategic` and tell the owner in `#team-support`. A T1 agent who picks one up by mistake may send the first response, so that the clock is met, and then hands over.

## What does not change

Strategic status changes who answers and how fast we call. It does not change the rules:

- the refund window and the approval of large refunds are the same as for every merchant ([Handling merchant refund requests](refunds.md));
- the dispute process, its deadlines and its fee are the same ([Chargebacks and disputes](chargebacks.md));
- KYC reviews apply in full.

Some strategic merchants have contract terms that differ from our standard ones. Support does not interpret contracts. If a merchant cites their contract, on liability, service levels or anything else, send the question to Legal at `legal@quillmoor.example` and tell the merchant that it has been passed on. Background for Legal's side is in the [MSA clause guide](../legal/msa-clause-guide.md).

## Owners' checklist

- Read every new ticket from your merchants on the day it arrives, even if a colleague is handling it.
- Keep the account notes in Harbour current: contacts, integrations, open issues, anything the deputy needs to cover for you.
- Before the monthly call, look at the merchant's tickets, disputes and payout failures for the month, and at any incidents that affected them.
- When you are on annual leave, tell the merchant who your deputy is, and hand over open tickets in an internal note.
