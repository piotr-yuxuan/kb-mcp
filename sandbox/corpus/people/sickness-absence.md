## If you are ill today

1. **Tell your manager by 10:00** on your first day of absence, by phone or by a
   direct chat message. A message in a team channel alone is not enough.
2. Say, roughly, when you expect to be back. You do not have to say what is wrong.
3. If you are on call, or have a meeting with a merchant today, say so, so that
   someone can cover it.
4. Record the absence in Haven, today if you can, otherwise on your first day back.
5. Rest.

The rest of this page explains the rules behind those five steps, how sick pay works
and how we support longer absences.

## Telling us

We ask you to tell your manager by 10:00 so that the team can rearrange the day:
cover a rota, move a meeting, warn a merchant. If you cannot reach your manager, tell
their manager or the People team. If you are too unwell to contact anyone, ask
someone to do it for you.

If you are unwell for more than one day, update your manager when your expected
return date changes. You do not need to call in every day once your manager knows the
likely length of your absence.

If you start work and feel too unwell to continue, tell your manager and stop. A day
on which you worked for less than half of your hours is recorded as half a day of
sickness.

### If you are on call

Tell your manager and hand over your on-call shift as soon as you know you cannot
answer a page. The [on-call rota and paging policy](../incidents/on-call.md) explains
how cover is arranged; nobody is expected to carry the pager while ill.

## Evidence: self-certification and fit notes

| Length of absence | What you provide |
|---|---|
| 1 to 7 calendar days | A self-certification in Haven, on your first day back |
| More than 7 calendar days | A fit note from a doctor, covering the absence from the 8th calendar day |

Calendar days include weekends and bank holidays, even if you do not normally work on
them.

Upload fit notes in Haven, as a photo or a scan. Only the People team can see the
document; your manager sees the dates and nothing else.

A fit note may say that you "may be fit for work" with some changes, such as shorter
hours or no travel. If it does, your manager and the People team will talk to you
about whether those changes are possible. If they are, you return on that basis; if
not, the fit note is treated as saying that you are not fit for work.

### Worked examples

**A short absence.** Maria falls ill on Thursday 5 February 2026 and comes back on
Tuesday 10 February. Her absence covers five calendar days, from Thursday to Monday,
so she self-certifies in Haven on Tuesday. She has used three working days of sick
pay.

**A longer absence.** Elliot falls ill on Monday 9 March 2026 and comes back on
Wednesday 18 March. His absence covers nine calendar days, so he needs a fit note from
Monday 16 March, the 8th day. He self-certifies the first seven days and uploads the
fit note for the rest. He has used seven working days of sick pay.

## Sick pay

### Full pay

You receive **10 days at full pay a year** for sickness absence. The year is the
calendar year, like the leave year: the count goes back to zero on 1 January. Part-time
colleagues receive the same number of their own working days.

Haven counts the days for you and shows how many you have left under "My absences".

### After the 10 days

Further sickness absence in the same year is paid at statutory sick pay, if you
qualify for it, which the payroll team checks. If you do not qualify, it is unpaid.

**Worked example.** By the end of September 2026, Kofi has had seven days of sickness
absence. He is then ill from Monday 5 to Friday 9 October 2026, five working days.
The first three are at full pay, which brings him to ten; the last two are paid at
statutory sick pay.

### Serious or long-term illness

For serious illness, an operation, or any absence expected to last more than four
weeks, the Head of people can extend full pay beyond the 10 days, and then half pay.
We decide case by case and as early as possible, so that you know where you stand. We
have never yet refused an extension for a serious illness.

After 26 weeks of continuous absence, our income protection insurance may take over,
paying a proportion of your salary for as long as you cannot work. The People team
makes the claim with you; it is described in [Benefits in the UK](benefits-uk.md).

### What does not count towards the 10 days

- Absence covered by an extension agreed by the Head of people.
- Pregnancy-related illness, which is recorded separately and never affects your sick
  pay.
- Absence related to a disability, where we have agreed it as a reasonable adjustment.
- Medical appointments (see below).

## Coming back to work

After any absence of more than three working days, your manager will have a short
return-to-work conversation with you on your first day back. It is not a disciplinary
meeting and not a medical assessment. Its purpose is to:

- check that you are well enough to be back;
- tell you what happened while you were away;
- agree any temporary changes, such as lighter work or later starts for a week;
- confirm that the absence is recorded in Haven correctly.

### A phased return

After a long absence, or when your doctor suggests it, you can return gradually: for
example, three days a week for two weeks, then four, then your full hours. During an
agreed phased return you receive full pay for up to four weeks. Your manager and the
People team agree the plan with you, in writing.

### Occupational health

If an absence lasts more than four weeks, or if you have repeated absences for the
same reason, we may suggest a referral to an independent occupational health adviser.
The adviser helps us understand what support or adjustments would help you, not your
diagnosis. You see the report before we do, and you can ask for corrections.

## Repeated short absences

Everyone is ill sometimes, and we do not count absences to catch people out. However,
if you have four or more separate absences within 12 months, your manager will ask to
talk to you, because a pattern can point to something we could help with: a health
condition, a workload problem, a difficult commute, caring responsibilities. The
conversation is supportive, and the People team can join it if you wish.

## Related situations

| Situation | How it is handled |
|---|---|
| You fall ill during annual leave | Tell your manager and record the sickness in Haven; the leave days are returned to your balance. See the [Annual leave policy (UK)](annual-leave-uk.md). |
| Medical, dental or hospital appointments | Not sickness absence. Try to book them at the start or end of the day and agree the time with your manager. |
| Your child or a dependant is ill | Not sickness absence. You can take paid emergency leave for dependants to make arrangements; talk to your manager. |
| You are ill while working from abroad | Same rules. Tell your manager by 10:00 your local time. See [Working from abroad](working-abroad.md). |
| Mental health | Treated exactly like any other illness. See also [Wellbeing support](wellbeing.md). |
| Injury at work | Report it to a first aider and record it in the accident book; see [First aiders and the wellbeing room](../workplace/first-aiders.md). |
| Infectious illness | Stay at home and work only if you feel well enough and your manager agrees. Do not come to the office until you are no longer infectious. |

## Your privacy

Information about your health is sensitive personal data. We keep fit notes,
occupational health reports and any medical information in Haven, visible only to the
People team. Your manager sees the dates of your absence and whether it has been
certified. We never share health information with your team without your agreement.

## France

Colleagues employed by Quillmoor SAS follow the same first step: tell your manager by
10:00. After that, French rules apply:

- If a doctor gives you a sick note (arrêt de travail), send it to your health
  insurance fund within 48 hours, as the law requires, and a copy to the People team
  through Haven.
- Quillmoor maintains your full salary from the first day of the arrêt de travail,
  without the statutory waiting days, and receives the social security daily
  allowances in your place. The UK rule of 10 days at full pay does not apply to you.
- Longer absences are covered by the company's prévoyance; see
  [Mutuelle et prévoyance (France)](mutuelle-et-prevoyance.md).
- Leave that you could not take because of sickness is carried over under French law;
  see [Congés payés et RTT (France)](conges-payes-rtt.md).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I work from home instead of taking a sick day?**
If you are well enough to work and would simply be better at home, for example with a
cold, agree it with your manager: that is a working day, not sickness. If you are not
well enough to work, take the sick day. Please do not work while ill to save your sick
pay.

**Do I need a fit note if I fall ill on a Friday and come back on Monday?**
No. That is four calendar days at most, so you self-certify.

**I forgot to tell my manager by 10:00 because I was asleep. Will I be in trouble?**
No. Tell them as soon as you can. The rule exists so that the team can plan, not to
catch anyone out.

**Can my manager ask what is wrong with me?**
They can ask how you are, and whether there is anything the team can do. You do not
have to tell them your diagnosis.

**Does sickness affect my annual leave?**
Annual leave keeps building up while you are off sick, and leave you could not take
because of long-term sickness can be carried over beyond the usual limit.

**Where do I find my remaining days at full pay?**
In Haven, under "My absences". If the count looks wrong, write to
`people@quillmoor.example`.
