---
title: "Learning budget"
---

Every employee has a personal learning budget of **£1,000 a year** to spend on their
professional development. It is yours to use; you agree what to spend it on with your
manager, who will rarely say no to anything that helps you grow in your role or
towards the next one.

Owner: Rhiannon Dace, Head of people. Budget holder: your department.
Last reviewed: 4 May 2026.

## The rules in short

- £1,000 for each fiscal year, from 1 April to 31 March.
- New joiners receive the full amount, whatever their start date.
- Part-time colleagues receive the full amount too.
- Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.
- Colleagues on French contracts have the same budget, converted to euros at the rate
  Finance sets each April.

## What it covers

| Usually yes | Usually no |
|---|---|
| Courses, online or in person | Travel and hotels for a conference (these come from your team's travel budget) |
| Conference tickets | Degrees or long qualifications (ask the People team about study support instead) |
| Professional certifications and exam fees | Equipment, such as a laptop or monitor |
| Books, including e-books and audiobooks | Subscriptions that the company already provides |
| Coaching or mentoring from an external professional | Personal interests unrelated to any role at Quillmoor |
| Language lessons, where a language helps in your role | |

If you are unsure, ask your manager before you spend anything. When a request does
not fit either column, managers can ask the People team, which keeps a list of past
decisions so that we stay consistent.

## How to spend it

1. Agree the purchase with your manager, in a one-to-one or by message. A short note
   in Haven, under "My learning", is enough to record the approval.
2. For anything under £150, such as books or a short online course, buy it yourself
   and claim it back, following the [Expenses policy](../finance/expenses.md).
3. For anything more expensive, ask Finance to pay the provider directly by raising
   a purchase order; see [Raising a purchase order in Tally](../finance/purchase-orders.md).
   This saves you from carrying a large amount on your own card.
4. Log what you learnt in Haven afterwards, in a sentence or two. It helps at your
   [performance review](performance-reviews.md).

## Time to learn

Time spent on agreed courses, conferences and exams is working time; you do not use
annual leave for it. Agree the dates with your manager as you would for any other
absence from the team.

## Sharing the budget

Several people in a team can pool their budgets for a larger item, such as a
workshop run for the whole team, with the department head's approval. Each person's
share is recorded in Haven.

## If you leave

We do not ask you to repay anything you spent from your learning budget, whenever you
leave. Courses booked but not yet taken when you hand in your notice are cancelled
where possible.
