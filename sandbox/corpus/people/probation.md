title: "Probation period"
---

Everyone who joins Quillmoor starts with a **probation period of 3 months**. It is a
chance for you and for us to check that the role is right, with regular, honest
conversations along the way. The vast majority of probation periods end in a simple
confirmation.

*Owner: Rhiannon Dace, Head of people. Last reviewed: 16 March 2026.*

## How it works

| When | What happens | Recorded in Haven |
|---|---|---|
| Day 10 | Two-week review: you and your manager agree written goals for the rest of probation | Goals |
| End of month 1 | Check-in: progress against the goals, what help you need | Short note |
| End of month 2 | Check-in: same, and an early, frank view of how things are going | Short note |
| Last two weeks of month 3 | End-of-probation review | Outcome |

The two-week review is part of your onboarding; see
[New joiner onboarding: your first two weeks](onboarding.md).

If something is not going well, your manager should say so clearly at the month 1 or
month 2 check-in, with specific examples and the support on offer. Nothing said at the
final review should come as a surprise.

## The outcome

At the end-of-probation review, your manager decides one of three outcomes, confirmed
by the People team:

1. **Confirmed.** You receive a confirmation letter in Haven. This is by far the most
   common outcome.
2. **Extended.** Where there is a real chance of success with more time, probation can
   be extended once, by up to six weeks, with written goals for the extension.
3. **Not confirmed.** Your employment ends, with the notice below. A member of the
   People team is present at this meeting.

If your manager has not held the review by the end of the 3 months, tell the People
team. Your probation is not extended automatically.

## During probation

- **Notice.** For UK contracts, the notice period during probation is one week on
  either side. After confirmation, the notice period in your contract applies.
- **Annual leave.** You can take annual leave as usual.
- **Absence.** Probation pauses during parental leave and during sickness absence of
  more than two weeks, and resumes on your return.
- **Benefits.** Most benefits start on day one. The cycle-to-work scheme and the
  season ticket loan start after confirmation; see [Benefits in the UK](benefits-uk.md).
- **Performance reviews.** While you are in probation, your probation reviews replace
  the company cycle; see [Performance reviews](performance-reviews.md).

## France

For colleagues on French contracts, probation is the période d'essai set out in your
contract, whose length depends on your job category under French law. French law also
sets the rules on renewal and on notice (délai de prévenance), which differ from the
UK rules above. Noémie Ferrand, HR adviser for France, can explain them.

## For managers

- Book the check-ins in your calendar on the joiner's first day.
- Write a few lines in Haven after each one, even when all is well.
- Talk to the People team as soon as you have concerns, not at the end.
- Hold the final review on time. A late review is unfair to the joiner and creates
  uncertainty for both of you.
