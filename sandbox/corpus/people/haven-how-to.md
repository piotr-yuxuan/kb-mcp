title: "Using Haven"
---

Haven is our HR system. You use it to request leave, record sickness, read your
payslips and keep your personal details up to date. Managers use it to approve
requests and to see their team's calendar. This page is a practical guide to the
tasks people ask about most; for the rules behind them, follow the links to the
policies.

*Owner: People team. Haven administrator: the payroll team. Last reviewed: 1 October
2026.*

## Signing in

- **On the web:** `https://haven.quillmoor.example`, with your company account and
  your second factor.
- **On your phone:** the Haven mobile app, which you find by searching for "Haven
  Quillmoor" in your phone's app store. Sign in with your company account the first
  time; after that, the app unlocks with your phone's own screen lock.
- **Before your first day:** you sign in with the invitation sent to your personal
  email. On day one, your account switches to your company identity, and the
  invitation link stops working.

If you cannot sign in, check first that your company account works elsewhere. If it
does, write to `people@quillmoor.example`; if it does not, contact the IT service
desk.

## The home page

The home page shows four panels:

| Panel | What it shows |
|---|---|
| My leave | Entitlement for the year, carried days with their expiry date, days booked and days remaining |
| My absences | Sickness absence this year and the days at full pay remaining |
| My pay | Your latest payslip and the next pay date |
| To do | Anything waiting for you: a document to sign, a review to write, an approval |

## Requesting leave

1. Select **Request absence**.
2. Choose the type:
   - **Annual leave** (UK contracts);
   - **Congés payés** or **RTT** (French contracts);
   - **Work from abroad**;
   - **Compassionate leave**, **Jury service**, **Emergency leave for dependants**;
   - **Parental leave** types appear only after you have spoken to the People team.
3. Pick the dates. For a half day, choose morning or afternoon.
4. Check the number of days Haven calculates. It already excludes weekends, bank
   holidays and days you do not normally work.
5. Add a note for your manager if useful, then **Submit**.

Your manager receives an email and a notification in the app. You receive an email
when they decide. The request then appears in your team's calendar, showing only
"Away", never the type of absence.

The rules for each type are in the policies: the
[Annual leave policy (UK)](annual-leave-uk.md),
[Congés payés et RTT (France)](conges-payes-rtt.md) and
[Working from abroad](working-abroad.md).

### Cancelling or changing a request

Open the request under **My leave**, then **Cancel**. If the leave has been approved,
your manager is told; nothing else is needed. To change dates, cancel and submit a
new request.

Leave that has already started cannot be cancelled in Haven. If you come back early,
ask your manager to adjust it, and the remaining days go back to your balance.

## Recording sickness

Select **Record sickness**, enter the first day, and either the last day or "Still
off". When you are back:

- for a short absence, complete the self-certification that Haven shows you;
- for a longer one, upload the fit note (a photo is fine) under **Documents**.

Fit notes are visible only to the People team; your manager sees the dates. The rules
are on [Sickness absence](sickness-absence.md).

## Pay and documents

- **Payslips:** **My pay**, then **Payslips**. They appear a few days before the pay
  date; see [Payroll dates](payroll-dates.md).
- **Tax documents:** under **My pay**, then **Year-end documents**.
- **Contract and letters:** under **Documents**, with anything you have signed in
  Haven.
- **Bank details:** **My details**, then **Bank account**. A change is confirmed by a
  call from the payroll team before it takes effect.

## Keeping your details up to date

Please check **My details** once a year, and whenever something changes: your home
address, emergency contacts, life assurance nominations, or your name. Changes of
address must be made in Haven, because payroll uses it for official documents.

## For managers

### Approving requests

Requests waiting for you appear under **To do**, and in the email you receive. Open a
request to see the team calendar for the same dates, then **Approve** or
**Decline**. If you decline, Haven asks for a reason, which the requester sees.

### Delegating

If you will be away, delegate approvals under **Settings**, then **Delegate**, to
another manager for a set period. The delegate sees only requests, not your team's
sickness details.

### Team calendar

**My team**, then **Calendar**, shows who is away and when. Sickness and parental
leave appear as "Away" to the team; you see the type for your own reports.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| My balance looks wrong | Check carried days and their expiry under **My leave**, then write to `people@quillmoor.example` |
| I submitted a request for the wrong type | Cancel it and submit again |
| My manager has left and requests go nowhere | The People team reassigns your approver within a day |
| The app shows old data | Pull down to refresh, or sign out and back in |
| I cannot see the "Work from abroad" type | It appears once you have completed your first week |

Haven is reached through our single sign-on, so the usual rules for your company
account apply; see the [Password and MFA standard](../security/password-and-mfa.md).
