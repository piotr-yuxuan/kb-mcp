title: "Employee referral scheme"
---

The best people we hire often come through someone who already works here. If you
refer someone and we hire them, you receive a **referral bonus of £2,000**.

Owner: Rhiannon Dace, Head of people. Last reviewed: 10 August 2026.

## How to refer someone

1. Find the role on the careers page at `careers.quillmoor.example`, or ask the
   hiring manager about roles not yet published.
2. Ask the person whether they are happy to be referred. Never submit someone without
   their agreement.
3. In Haven, open "Refer a candidate" and attach their CV, or the link to their
   public profile, with a few lines on how you know them and why you think they would
   do well.
4. The recruiting team contacts them within a week and keeps you informed of the
   main steps. For privacy reasons we cannot share interview feedback with you.

## When the bonus is paid

The bonus is paid in the payroll after your referral **completes their probation
period**; see [Probation period](probation.md) and [Payroll dates](payroll-dates.md).
You must still be employed by Quillmoor on that date.

The £2,000 is paid through payroll, so income tax and National Insurance are
deducted. Colleagues on the French payroll receive the euro equivalent, converted at
the rate on the payment date, with the usual social contributions.

## Who can take part

Everyone at Quillmoor can refer, with these exceptions, which avoid conflicts of
interest:

| Not eligible | Why |
|---|---|
| The hiring manager for the role, and anyone on that role's interview panel | They decide the hire |
| The People team, including recruiters | Finding candidates is their job |
| The executive team | Same reason as hiring managers, across the company |

Referring a relative or partner is allowed, but tell the recruiting team, who will
make sure you are not involved in the decision.

## What counts as a referral

- The candidate must be new to our recruiting system: if they applied directly, or
  through an agency, in the previous 12 months, the earlier application stands.
- If two people refer the same candidate, the first referral recorded in Haven
  counts.
- Rehiring a former employee counts, if they left more than a year ago.
- Contractors converted to employees do not count.

## Making a good referral

A referral is most useful when it tells the hiring team something a CV cannot.

- Say how you know the person and for how long: a former teammate whose work you saw
  every day is a stronger signal than a contact from a meetup.
- Say what they are good at, with an example if you have one.
- Be honest about what you do not know. "I have never seen them lead a team" is
  helpful, not disloyal.
- Refer people for roles that fit them. A strong engineer referred to a role two
  levels above their experience has a poor experience of us, and so do we.

The bonus is the same whether or not you know the person well; the quality of your
note is what makes the difference to their chances.
