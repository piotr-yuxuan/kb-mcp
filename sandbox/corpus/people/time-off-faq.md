Quick answers to the questions the People team hears most often about holidays,
sick days and other time away from work. This page deliberately gives no figures:
entitlements, limits and deadlines live in the policies, which are the only
authoritative source. Each answer tells you which one to read.

*Maintained by the People team. Last reviewed: 15 October 2026.*

## Holidays and annual leave

**How much holiday do I get?**
It depends on your contract. On a UK contract, read the
[Annual leave policy (UK)](annual-leave-uk.md). On a French contract, read
[Congés payés et RTT (France)](conges-payes-rtt.md). Your own balance, including
anything pro rata for a part-time pattern or a mid-year start, is on the Haven home
page.

**Are bank holidays included in my allowance?**
For UK contracts, bank holidays come on top of your annual leave; the policy explains
how this works for part-time patterns. French public holidays are covered in the
French page.

**Can I carry unused holiday over into next year?**
Some of it, for a limited time. The limit and the deadline are different in the UK
and in France, so check the policy for your contract. Haven shows any carried days
with the date they expire.

**I found a page called "Annual leave policy 2022 (UK)". Does it still apply?**
No. It was replaced in January 2024 and is kept only for reference. Always use the
current [Annual leave policy (UK)](annual-leave-uk.md).

**How far ahead do I need to book a long vacation?**
The notice rules are in the policy for your contract. In short: the longer the
absence, the earlier you should ask, and summer requests in France are planned in
the spring.

**How do I book time off?**
In Haven, from the web or the mobile app. The steps are in
[Using Haven](haven-how-to.md). Your manager approves the request there.

**My manager has not answered my request. What should I do?**
Remind them; Haven also sends them reminders. If you still have no answer, ask your
manager's manager, or the People team.

**What is RTT, and do I get it?**
RTT days exist only for colleagues on French contracts. The French page explains
what they are and who receives them.

## Sickness

**I am ill. Who do I tell, and by when?**
Your manager, on the first morning. The deadline and the rest of the process are on
[Sickness absence](sickness-absence.md).

**When do I need a doctor's note?**
Only after a certain number of days; short absences are self-certified in Haven. See
[Sickness absence](sickness-absence.md).

**I got ill during my holiday. Can I have the days back?**
Yes, if you tell your manager and record the sickness in Haven. Both the UK and the
French rules return the days to you.

## Family and personal reasons

**I am expecting a baby, or adopting. Where do I start?**
With the [Parental leave policy](parental-leave.md), and a confidential note to
`people@quillmoor.example` whenever you are ready.

**Someone close to me has died.**
We are sorry. Tell your manager, or ask a colleague to tell them for you. You can take
paid compassionate leave, which never comes out of your annual leave; the People team
will explain what is available and help with anything else you need.

**My child is ill and I need to stay home.**
That is emergency leave for dependants, not annual leave or sickness. Tell your
manager; it is covered in [Sickness absence](sickness-absence.md).

**I have been called for jury service.**
Tell your manager and send the summons to the People team. You are paid as normal and
it does not use your annual leave.

**Can I take time off for a religious festival that is not a bank holiday?**
Yes: book it as annual leave in the usual way. Managers are asked to give these
requests priority when several people ask for the same dates.

**Can I take unpaid leave or a sabbatical?**
Possibly. Requests are considered case by case, depending on the reason and on your
team. Talk to your manager first, then the People team.

## Working elsewhere

**Can I work from abroad during a holiday?**
Working days spent abroad are not annual leave, and they are limited. Read
[Working from abroad](working-abroad.md) before you book anything.

**I am on call during my holiday dates.**
Swap your shifts before your leave is approved; the
[on-call rota and paging policy](../incidents/on-call.md) explains how.

## Leaving

**What happens to my unused holiday when I leave?**
It is settled in your final pay, either way. See [Leaving Quillmoor](leaving.md) and
the policy for your contract.

## Still stuck?

Ask your manager, or write to `people@quillmoor.example`. If your question is likely
to come up again, we will add it here.
