## Pension

Every UK employee is enrolled in the company pension scheme on their first day.

- **Quillmoor contributes 6% of your basic salary**, every month.
- You contribute 3% of your basic salary by default. You can raise your contribution
  at any time in Haven, with changes applied from the next payroll.
- Contributions are made through **salary sacrifice**: your salary is reduced by your
  contribution and Quillmoor pays the same amount into your pension. You pay less tax
  and National Insurance as a result. If salary sacrifice does not suit you, for
  example because you are applying for a mortgage, you can switch to ordinary
  deductions.
- You choose your investments in the online account of our pension provider. If you
  do not choose, your savings go into the scheme's default fund.

You can opt out within one month of enrolment and receive back what you paid in. We
would encourage you to think carefully first: opting out means giving up the
employer contribution as well. The law requires us to enrol you again every three
years, and you can rejoin whenever you like.

Pension contributions continue on your normal salary during paid parental leave; see
the [Parental leave policy](parental-leave.md).

## Private medical insurance

Our medical insurer covers you from your first day, with no medical questions asked:
conditions you had before joining are covered after two years without symptoms or
treatment.

- **Covered:** specialist consultations, diagnostic tests, hospital treatment, cancer
  care, and mental health treatment, including therapy sessions on referral.
- **Not covered:** routine dental care, maternity care, and chronic conditions after
  initial treatment.
- **Excess:** £100 per claim year.
- **Family:** you can add a partner and children in Haven. Quillmoor pays your own
  cover; you pay for family members through payroll.

Private medical insurance is a taxable benefit: it appears on your annual statement
of benefits and, for most people, adds a small amount of tax through your tax code.

To make a claim, call the insurer's helpline from the membership details in Haven
before booking any treatment.

## Life assurance and income protection

Life assurance pays four times your basic salary to the people you nominate. Keep
your nomination up to date in Haven, especially after a change in your family.

Income protection pays part of your salary if illness or injury keeps you off work for
more than 26 weeks in a row, after company sick pay. The People team makes the claim
with you; how sick pay works before that is on [Sickness absence](sickness-absence.md).

## When benefits end

Benefits stop on your last day of employment. You can usually continue your private
medical insurance individually, without new medical questions, if you contact the
insurer within a month. Your pension stays yours. More on
[Leaving Quillmoor](leaving.md).
