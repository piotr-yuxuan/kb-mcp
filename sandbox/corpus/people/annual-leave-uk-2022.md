## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy explains the annual leave entitlement of employees of Quillmoor Ltd
in the United Kingdom, how leave is requested and approved, and how unused leave is
treated at the end of the leave year.

1.2 It aims to make sure that everyone takes enough rest during the year, that teams
can plan cover, and that entitlements are applied consistently.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy applies to all employees on a UK contract of employment, whether
full-time or part-time, permanent or fixed-term, including employees in their
probation period.

2.2 It does not apply to contractors, consultants or agency workers, whose leave
arrangements are set by their own agreements.

2.3 Employees on a French contract are covered by the French leave rules, which are
documented separately.

## 3. Definitions

3.1 **Leave year**: the calendar year, from 1 January to 31 December.

3.2 **Working day**: a day on which you are contracted to work. For full-time
employees, Monday to Friday.

3.3 **Bank holidays**: the public bank holidays in the part of the United Kingdom
named in your contract.

3.4 **Closure days**: the working days between Christmas and New Year on which the
company closes its offices (see clause 5).

## 4. Entitlement

4.1 Full-time employees are entitled to **25 days of annual leave in each leave
year**, plus bank holidays.

4.2 The 25 days include the closure days described in clause 5. In 2022 and 2023
there were 3 closure days each year, leaving 22 days to book freely.

4.3 Part-time employees are entitled to a pro rata share of the 25 days and of the
bank holidays, based on the number of days they are contracted to work each week.
Entitlements are rounded up to the nearest half day.

4.4 Employees who join or leave during the leave year are entitled to a pro rata
share of the 25 days, calculated by complete and partial months of service and
rounded up to the nearest half day.

4.5 Entitlement accrues from the first day of employment. Employees may take leave
during their probation period.

### Table 1: examples of entitlement under clause 4

| Situation | Calculation | Entitlement |
|---|---|---|
| Full-time, whole year | 25 days | 25 days plus bank holidays |
| Three days a week, whole year | 25 × 3 / 5 = 15 days; bank holidays 8 × 3 / 5 = 4.8 | 15 days plus 5 days for bank holidays |
| Full-time, joined 1 June 2022 | 25 × 7 / 12 = 14.58 | 15 days |
| Full-time, left 30 September 2022 | 25 × 9 / 12 = 18.75 | 19 days |

## 5. Closure between Christmas and New Year

5.1 The company closes its offices on the working days between the Christmas
bank holidays and the New Year bank holidays. Employees use annual leave for those days.

5.2 The People team adds the closure days to every employee's Haven calendar in
January, so that they are reserved from the start of the year.

5.3 Employees who are required to work on a closure day, for example to provide
on-call cover, keep those days in their balance and may book them at another time.

5.4 Employees who have not accrued enough leave to cover the closure days, because
they joined late in the year, are not asked to take unpaid leave: the shortfall is
written off.

## 6. Carry-over

6.1 Employees may **carry over up to 5 days** of unused annual leave from one leave
year into the next.

6.2 Carried-over days **must be used by 28 February** of the following leave year.
Days not used by that date are lost.

6.3 Carry-over is not automatic. Employees must request it in Haven by 15 December,
with their manager's approval. Requests received after 15 December are considered
only in exceptional circumstances. (Clarified in November 2022: the request is for
the number of days, not for specific dates.)

6.4 Unused days in excess of 5 are lost at the end of the leave year and are not
paid.

6.5 The limits in 6.1 and 6.2 do not apply where an employee could not take leave
because of sickness or family leave. In those cases, the People team will agree a
carry-over in line with the law.

### Example under clause 6

On 31 December 2022 an employee has 7 days unused and requested carry-over in time.

1. Five days are carried into 2023; two are lost.
2. The employee books 3 days in January 2023.
3. On 28 February 2023 the 2 remaining carried days are lost.

## 7. Requesting leave

7.1 All annual leave must be requested in Haven and approved by your line manager
before it is taken.

7.2 For an absence of **two weeks or more**, give at least **four weeks' notice**.
For shorter absences, give at least twice as many days' notice as the number of
days requested, wherever possible.

7.3 Managers should respond within five working days. A manager may decline a
request where the absence would affect the operation of the team. Requests are
generally considered in the order in which they are received.

7.4 No more than 10 consecutive working days of annual leave may be taken at once
without the approval of your manager's manager.

7.5 Approved leave may be cancelled by the employee at any time before it starts.
The company may cancel approved leave only in exceptional business circumstances,
and will reimburse any costs that cannot be recovered.

### Example under clause 7

An employee wants to take Monday 1 August to Friday 12 August 2022, which is ten
working days and therefore two weeks. Under clause 7.2 the request should be made by
Monday 4 July 2022. Because it is not more than 10 consecutive working days, clause
7.4 does not apply.

## 8. Sickness during annual leave

8.1 An employee who falls ill during annual leave should notify their manager on the
first day of illness, following the sickness absence procedure.

8.2 Days of sickness covered by medical evidence may be reinstated as annual leave.
Self-certified days during annual leave are not reinstated.

## 9. Leave on termination

9.1 On leaving, an employee is paid for accrued but untaken leave for the current
leave year, plus any valid carried-over days.

9.2 Leave taken in excess of accrued entitlement is deducted from final pay.

9.3 The company may require an employee to take some or all of their outstanding
leave during their notice period.

### Example under clause 9

An employee leaves on 30 September 2022, with an entitlement of 19 days (table 1)
including the 3 closure days that would have fallen in December.

- Having taken 12 days, the employee is paid for 7 days.
- Having taken 21 days, the employee has 2 days deducted from final pay.

Because the employee leaves before the closure, the closure days stay in the
calculation as ordinary days and are paid or deducted like any other.

## 10. Pay during annual leave

10.1 Employees are paid their normal basic salary during annual leave.

10.2 Regular overtime and allowances are included in pay for leave days where the law
requires it.

## 11. Responsibilities

| Who | Responsibility under this policy |
|---|---|
| Employees | Plan and request leave in good time; request carry-over by 15 December; record sickness during leave |
| Line managers | Respond to requests promptly; make sure their team takes its leave through the year; approve or decline carry-over |
| People team | Maintain balances in Haven; publish the closure days each January; advise on exceptions |
| Payroll | Settle leave balances in final pay |

## 13. Questions raised in 2022

These questions were collected by the People team during the first year of this
version and answered at the time. They are kept as they were written.

**Do the closure days count when I calculate the 5 days I may carry over?**
No. The closure days are reserved in January, so they are never part of your unused
balance. Only days still free on 31 December can be carried over.

**I forgot to request carry-over by 15 December. Is it too late?**
Ask your manager to email the People team before 31 December, explaining why. The
team decides case by case under clause 6.3; a late request is normally accepted once.

**Can I take my carried-over days in March if my project ends in February?**
No. Clause 6.2 is firm: carried-over days must be used by 28 February. Plan them
with your manager in January.

**I work four days a week. How are bank holidays handled?**
You receive 8 × 4 / 5 = 6.4 days for bank holidays, rounded up to 6.5, on top of
your 20 days of annual leave. When one of the bank holidays falls on a day you do not
work, you keep the time to book on another day.

**I am on call over Christmas. Do I still lose the closure days?**
No. Under clause 5.3 you keep them and may book them later in the year, subject to
the usual notice.

**Is there a minimum amount of leave I must take?**
Managers are asked to make sure that everyone takes at least two weeks during the
leave year. Haven sends a reminder in September to anyone who has booked less than
half of their entitlement.

## Appendix: what changed in the January 2024 review

This appendix was added when the policy was archived, so that readers arriving here
from an old link can find their way. The January 2024 policy:

- increased the entitlement;
- made carry-over automatic, with a higher limit and a later deadline;
- removed the compulsory closure between Christmas and New Year;
- replaced the notice rules in clauses 7.2 and 7.4 with a single, simpler rule;
- reinstated sickness days during annual leave whether or not they are self-certified.

Leave carried over from 2023 into 2024 was handled under the new rules. For every
current figure, read the [Annual leave policy (UK)](annual-leave-uk.md); for how leave
is booked today, see [Using Haven](haven-how-to.md).
