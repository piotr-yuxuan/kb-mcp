title: "Payroll dates"
---

Quillmoor pays salaries monthly, on the **25th of each month**, into the bank account
held in Haven. The same date applies to the UK payroll (Quillmoor Ltd) and the French
payroll (Quillmoor SAS).

*Owner: payroll team, part of People. Last reviewed: 12 January 2026.*

## When the 25th is not a working day

If the 25th falls on a weekend or a bank holiday, you are paid on the working day
before. December is the exception: we pay on the third Friday of the month, so that
pay arrives before the break.

## Cut-off for changes

Changes that affect your pay must be in Haven by the **10th of the month** to be
included in that month's pay. That covers new bank details, a change of hours,
pension contribution changes, and approvals for things paid through payroll, such as
keeping-in-touch days.

Anything approved after the 10th is paid the following month.

**Joiners.** If you start on or before the 10th, your first pay arrives on that
month's pay date. If you start after the 10th, your first pay arrives the following
month and includes the days you worked in your first month.

## Payslips and documents

- Your payslip appears in Haven, under "My pay", three working days before the pay
  date. You also receive an email when it is ready.
- UK colleagues find their annual P60 in Haven by the end of May, and their P45 there
  after leaving.
- French colleagues find their bulletins de paie in Haven, with a digital safe that
  keeps them after you leave.

Expense claims follow their own process and timetable: see the
[Expenses policy](../finance/expenses.md).

## Something wrong?

If your pay looks wrong, write to `people@quillmoor.example` with "Payroll" in the
subject, on the day you notice. We correct underpayments within three working days
by a separate transfer, rather than waiting for the next pay date.

Changes to your bank details are confirmed by a call to your work phone number. We
never ask for bank details by email, and nobody from payroll will ask you to change
them in a hurry: if someone does, report it as described in
[Reported phishing](../security/phishing.md).
