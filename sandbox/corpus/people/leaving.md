---
title: "Leaving Quillmoor"
---

Whatever your reason for leaving, we want your last weeks to be smooth and your
departure to be on good terms. This page lists what happens between your resignation
and your last day, and what you need to do. Managers have their own checklist at the
end.

Owner: Rhiannon Dace, Head of people. Last reviewed: 25 August 2026.

## Resigning

Tell your manager first, in person or on a call, then confirm your resignation in
writing: an email to your manager, copied to `people@quillmoor.example`, is enough.
Give your intended last working day. The People team confirms your leaving date and
any details in a letter in Haven, usually within three working days.

### Notice periods

Your notice period is in your contract. As a guide:

| Situation | Notice, on either side |
|---|---|
| During probation (UK) | One week; see [Probation period](probation.md) |
| Most roles after probation | One month |
| Senior roles (head of and above) | Three months |
| French contracts | As in your contract and under French law |

You and your manager can agree a shorter notice period if the handover allows.
Occasionally, we may ask you not to come to work during some or all of your notice,
called garden leave. You are still employed and paid as normal during garden leave.

## Your last pay

Your final pay arrives on the normal pay date after your last day; see
[Payroll dates](payroll-dates.md). It includes:

- your salary up to your last day;
- payment for annual leave you have not taken, or a deduction for leave taken beyond
  your entitlement, calculated as in the
  [Annual leave policy (UK)](annual-leave-uk.md) or, for French contracts, in
  [Congés payés et RTT (France)](conges-payes-rtt.md).

Your manager may ask you to take some outstanding leave during your notice instead.

UK leavers receive their P45 in Haven after the final pay. French leavers receive
their certificat de travail, solde de tout compte and the attestation for
unemployment benefits on their last day or shortly after.

## Expenses and company cards

Submit any outstanding expense claims before your last day, following the
[Expenses policy](../finance/expenses.md). If you hold a corporate card, it is
cancelled on your last day; see [Corporate cards](../finance/corporate-cards.md).

## Equipment

Return everything on your last day, or send it with the prepaid courier box that IT
sends to remote leavers:

- your laptop and its charger;
- your hardware security key, if you have one;
- your office pass (London) or badge (Paris);
- any monitor, headset or other equipment loaned to you for home working.

Do not wipe your laptop yourself: IT does it after checking that nothing needs
keeping. Before you go, move anything your team needs into shared folders and pages.
Personal files should not be on a company laptop, but if there are any, remove them
before your last day.

## Access

Your manager raises a JML leaver request as soon as your leaving date is confirmed.
It removes your access to every company system after your last day, on the timetable
set out in [Quarterly access reviews](../security/access-reviews.md). You do not need
to request anything yourself. Your email address is replaced by an automatic reply
pointing to your manager.

If you are on an on-call rota, your manager takes you off it before your last week.

## Benefits

- **Pension (UK).** Contributions stop with your final pay. Your pension stays yours;
  you can leave it where it is or transfer it.
- **Private medical insurance (UK).** Cover ends on your last day. You can continue it
  individually if you contact the insurer within a month; see
  [Benefits in the UK](benefits-uk.md).
- **Mutuelle and prévoyance (France).** Usually continue free of charge for a period
  after you leave, under the portabilité rules; see
  [Mutuelle et prévoyance (France)](mutuelle-et-prevoyance.md).
- **Learning budget.** Nothing to repay.

## Your exit conversation

In your last two weeks, someone from the People team invites you to an exit
conversation of about half an hour. It is optional and separate from your manager.
We ask what worked, what did not, and what would have made you stay. Answers are
reported to the leadership team only in aggregate, once there are enough leavers in a
quarter to keep them anonymous.

## References

The People team gives factual references only: your dates of employment and your
last job title. Managers may give personal references in their own name, if they
wish, but not on behalf of Quillmoor. Send reference requests to
`people@quillmoor.example`.

## Coming back

Many people who leave Quillmoor come back. If you would like to return one day, tell
us at your exit conversation and we will keep in touch. A former employee who rejoins
after less than a year keeps their original start date for service-related purposes.

## For managers: leaver checklist

1. Acknowledge the resignation in writing and forward it to the People team the same
   day.
2. Raise the JML leaver request with the confirmed last day.
3. Agree a handover plan and share it with the team.
4. Check the leave balance in Haven and decide whether outstanding leave is taken or
   paid.
5. Take the leaver off any on-call or support rotas.
6. Arrange the return of equipment, or confirm that IT has sent the courier box.
7. Say goodbye properly: a team lunch or a message in the team channel goes a long way.
